You'll begin the game by controlling **Omegamon X** in a short battle against **Dexmon**. This teaches the basic combat system.

After the battle, complete the tutorial quests until you arrive at **DMW Village**.

## Important

Keep these items.

- Journey Exchange Ticket
- Starter Package Ticket

Do **not** use them yet.

>Ignore the **A New World** quest for now.
>
>New players cannot finish it yet.

Continue following the tutorial until **Teacher Mori** sends you back to **DMW Village**.

### LEVEL 120

After establishing your Jumping progression, work on leveling your Tamer and Digimon.

A strong early target is:
- Tamer Level 120
- Digimon Level 120

If you reach around Level 100 and leveling begins slowing down, look for other players farming on the samemap.

Joining a farming party can significantly improve your leveling speed.

Level 120 Digivice Dailies
After reaching Level 120, you will have access to two daily missions through your Digivice that can be used to help boost Digimon to Level 120.
:::warning[Reminder]
Remember to switch to the Digimon you want to level BEFORE claiming the reward.
:::

### DAILY PRIORITIES

Once you have started progressing, build a simple daily routine.
Claim Daily TicketsPress:
P → Claim Daily TicketsDo this every day.

Daily dungeon/content tickets accumulate into future progression opportunities, so forgetting them repeatedly will slow your account down over time.
Complete Useful Daily Missions Do the daily content relevant to your current progression.
You do not need to complete every activity in DMW immediately.
Prioritize content that advances your:
Digimon → Gear → Deck → Seals → Vice → Dungeon progression

---

### INVENTORY & WAREHOUSE

Do not immediately assume you need to spend money for every inventory upgrade.

Events frequently provide opportunities to obtain:
- Inventory Expansion
- Warehouse Expansion
- Archive Expansion

Keep an eye on active events and their rewards.
> Inventory space becomes increasingly important as you accumulate dungeon materials, tickets, seals, evolution items and equipment.

## DUNGEON PROGRESSION

After finishing the important Jumping progression and establishing your Digimon, role and basic equipment, you can begin moving deeper into DMW's dungeon progression.
The available DMW dungeon guides cover progression content including:

- Vajra
- Vika
- Chaotic
- Burning
- Final
- MDG
- PDG
- Mugen
- Pied
- Pillars
- APO
- Odaiba Park Extreme

**Do not interpret this simply as: “I unlocked it, therefore I am ready for it.”**

Before progressing into harder content, improve your Digimon, cloning, Deck, Seals, accessories and role-specific equipment.
Mechanics also become increasingly important.
A weaker player who understands the dungeon can often contribute more effectively than a stronger playerwho ignores mechanics.
