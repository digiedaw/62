---## Overview

By this point you should already have:

- Level 120 Starter Digimon
- Level 120 Journey Digimon
- Royal : X-Knight Bracelet
- Radiant Holy Beast Accessories
- Basic knowledge of dungeon mechanics

By the time you reach Mid Game, your goal is to prepare your account for End Game content.

Your goals are:

- Farm Fanglongmon Gear
- Obtain a free SSS+ Digimon
- Upgrade your Digivice
- Upgrade your clothing
- Prepare for End Game

---

## Progress Order

Follow this order for the smoothest progression.

- Daily Digimon Arena Hero/Extreme
- Fanglongmon Dungeon
- Obtain Free SSS+
- Upgrade Digivice
- Upgrade Clothing
- Prepare for End Game

---

## Digimon Arena

Hero Mode becomes your main daily activity.

Rewards include:

- Ranking Points
- Supreme Evolution Cards
- Materials for future SSS+ Digimon

You should level and fully clone all three Digimon:

- Vaccine
- Virus
- Data

Some later rounds require using different attributes.

Read the full guide:

## Get a Free SSS+

Once Fang Gear is finished, start working on your first free SSS+ Digimon.

#### Skill DPS

Recommended order:

1. Susanoomon Shin
2. Omegamon Alter-B
3. Fanglongmon Shin

#### Auto Attack DPS

Recommended order:

1. BloomLordmon
2. DarknessBagramon
3. Shoutmon X7

These Digimon will unlock much harder content.

### Fanglongmon Gear

Your next goal is replacing Holy Beast Gear with Fanglongmon Gear.

Complete your **Fanglongmon Gear** before moving into End Game.

Farm:
- FDG
- FDG SSS

Collect materials to craft:
- Ring
- Necklace
- Earring

Later you can upgrade them into Ultimate Ancient Gear.
:::warning[Do not skip this step.]
Fang Gear is the biggest power increase during Mid Game.
:::
---

## Upgrade Seals

At this stage, aim to unlock **1,000 seals** for every important stat.

This provides a significant increase in overall damage and survivability.

## Choose Your Main Role

By now, you should decide which role you want to invest in.

This determines:

- Which premium SSS+ Digimon to buy
- Which Digivice to upgrade
- Which accessory options to reroll

---

### ⚔️ Auto Attack (AA)

Goals:

- Buy **Zwart-D** or **Gaioumon: Itto Mode**
- Complete the **Corrupted Power** or **Knight of the Crimson Path** Deck
- Upgrade to **Uncontrollable D-Ark**

---

### ✨ Skill Damage (SK)

Goals:

- Buy **Omegamon X**
- Complete the **Latent Power** Deck
- Upgrade to **True Digivice**
- Upgrade to **Advanced True Digivice**

---

### 🛡️ Tank (TA)

Goals:

- Buy **Examon X**
- Complete the **Dragon Scale** Deck
- Upgrade to **Chrome Digizoid Vice**
