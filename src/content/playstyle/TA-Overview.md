# Tank / TA

> Tank builds focus on surviving encounters and handling the defensive side of party content.
Tank equipment is built differently from pure damage builds.

---

## Strengths

- High sustained DPS
- Easy to play
- Low DS consumption
- Excellent for long boss fights
- Great for farming

---

## Weaknesses

- Less burst damage
- Depends heavily on Attack Speed
- Requires strong accessories and seals

---

## Recommended For

- New players
- Farming
- Raid bosses
- Daily content
- Players who prefer simple rotations

---

## Core Stats Priority

1. Attack (AT)
2. Critical Damage (CD)
3. Attack Speed (AS)
4. Hit Rate (HT)
5. Critical Rate (CT)

---

## Progression

1. Build basic accessories.
2. Improve seals.
3. Upgrade equipment.
4. Unlock stronger Digimon.
5. Optimize Memory Skills.
