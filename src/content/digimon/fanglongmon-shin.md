## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — Fanglongmon Shin is unlocked from
**Qinglongmon + Baihumon + Zhuqiaomon + Xuanwumon**.

## Requirements 📋

- Fanglongmon unlocked
- 11× Lv.120 Baihumon
- 11× Lv.120 Zhuqiaomon
- 11× Lv.120 Xuanwumon
- 11× Lv.120 Qinglongmon

> 📍 Sacrifice the Lv.120 Digimon at the **Fanglongmon Shin NPC — DATS Center** to
> obtain their Fragments.

## Craft the 4 Cores 🧩

- ⚪ **Baihumon's Core** — 11× Baihumon's Fragment + 25× Flawless Crystal of Trace
- 🔴 **Zhuqiaomon's Core** — 11× Zhuqiaomon's Fragment + 25× Uprising Infernal Flame
  Crystal
- 🟢 **Xuanwumon's Core** — 11× Xuanwumon's Fragment + 25× Jack Frost of Water
  Crystal
- 🔵 **Qinglongmon's Core** — 11× Qinglongmon's Fragment + 50× Thunder God's
  Lightning Crystal

> The crystals come from their respective **Holy Beast Hard Mode Dungeons**.

## Fanglongmon Shin Package 🐲

After crafting all 4 Cores, you need:

- 1× Baihumon's Core
- 1× Zhuqiaomon's Core
- 1× Xuanwumon's Core
- 1× Qinglongmon's Core
- 200× Dragon Blood
- 30× Fanronkou
- 100T

> 📍 Craft the **Fanglongmon Shin Package** at **UlforceVeedramon FM — DATS Center**.

## Important 💡

- You need **44 Lv.120 Holy Beasts** total.
- Sacrificing them **permanently deletes** the Digimon.
- **Dragon Blood** comes from the Fanglongmon Shin Boss, summoned using **Supreme
  Evolution V2 Summon Cards**.
- The Fanglongmon Shin Package is **Character Bound**.

## Result ✅

**Qinglongmon + Baihumon + Zhuqiaomon + Xuanwumon** → ➡️ 🐉 **Fanglongmon Shin
Unlocked**
