---## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Rafflesimon is unlocked from **Lotusmon + Rosemon**
(Floramon + Palmon sides).

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 70+ Tamer
- Lv. 100+ Floramon

> 📍 Pick up **"A Call from Richard Sampson"** from **Calumon \** at
> **DATS Center**.

## Floramon Quest 🌸

1. Talk to **Richard Sampson → Yoshino, Thomas & Marcus** at DATS Center.
2. Go to **Lost Historic Site** → Collect:
   - 3× Yellow Tropical Plant
   - 3× Red Tropical Plant
   - 3× Purple Tropical Plant
3. Talk to **Palmon → Floramon** → Enter **Duel Sphere** and defeat
   **Floramon + Palmon**.
4. Continue the NPC quests through **Silver Lake → Lost Historic Site**.
5. Defeat:
   - 20× Waspmon
   - 20× Dokugumon
6. Enter Duel Sphere and defeat:
   - 2× CannonBeemon
   - 1× TigerVespamon
   - 2× Parasimon
   - Then 1× TyrantKabuterimon
7. Continue to **Silver Lake → Oil Refinery-2** and defeat:
   - 20× Guardromon
   - 20× Sinduramon
8. Enter Duel Sphere and defeat:
   - 5× Gizumon AT
   - Then 2× Chaosdramon + 2× Gizumon XT + 1× Gizumon AT
9. Return through **Silver Lake → DATS Center** and talk to
   **Richard Sampson → Yoshino → Marcus → White-Bearded Guru**.
10. Deliver the final quest item to **Nanomon**.

> 🎁 Receive **Rafflesimon: Data Core - Lotusmon**.

**✅ Floramon Side Complete**

## Palmon Side 🌹

There is no questline. You need:

- 🌹 **Rafflesimon: Data Core - Rosemon**

Use it to unlock Rafflesimon for **Palmon (Original)**. The item can be obtained
through **events** or limited-time **Cash Shop** availability.

## Important 💡

- 🌺 **Data Core - Lotusmon** = Floramon quest reward.
- 🌹 **Data Core - Rosemon** = Palmon side.
- You can use **any Digimon** for the required kills.
- If a Duel Sphere timer expires, **give up and retake the quest** for another
  Summon Card.
- Rafflesimon requires a **Jogress Chip**.

## Result ✅

**Lotusmon + Rosemon** → ➡️ 🌺 **Rafflesimon Unlocked**
