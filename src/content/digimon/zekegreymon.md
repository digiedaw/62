## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — ZekeGreymon is unlocked from **MailBirdramon + Greymon (C)** via
the **Evolution of Legend — Z** items.

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 70+ Tamer
- Lv. 70+ Greymon (C)
- MailBirdramon

## Questline ⚔️

1. Go to **Calumon** in DATS Center and begin the ZekeGreymon questline.

> 💡 Evolve Greymon (C) into **MetalGreymon (C)** to make the required quest
> available.

2. Travel to **Western Area — West** and speak with **Starsmon**.
3. Collect **30× Evolutionary Trail of Legend** by defeating:
   - **Coredramon (Blue)**
   - **Coredramon (Green)**
4. Progress through the required **Fanglongmon — Jogress Quest** encounter and
   return to the required NPC.
5. Continue and defeat **Fanglongmon Shin — Jogress Quest**, then return to
   Starsmon.

**✅ Questline Complete**

## Unlock Items 🔓

After completing the questline, Starsmon provides:

- 🔸 **Evolution of Legend — Z Alpha**
- 🔵 **Evolution of Legend — Z Beta**

These items are required to unlock the corresponding ZekeGreymon evolution slots.

## Unlock ✅

Use the required:

- 🔸 Evolution of Legend — Z Alpha
- 🔵 Evolution of Legend — Z Beta

to unlock **ZekeGreymon**.

## Important 💡

- **Greymon (C)** must meet the required level before starting.
- **MetalGreymon (C)** is required to activate the relevant quest progression.
- The **Evolutionary Trail of Legend** comes from **Coredramon (Blue)** and
  **Coredramon (Green)**.
- **Starsmon** can reissue the required reward if necessary.

## Result ✅

**MailBirdramon + Greymon (C)** → ➡️ 🦁 **ZekeGreymon Unlocked**
