## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — Leviamon X is Virus / Water, unlocked from **Leviamon** and is part
of the **IMD 3.0** progression.

## Requirements 📋

- Leviamon
- Leviamon X unlock item
- XAI

## IMD 3.0 🌊

1. Enter **📍 Infinite Mountain Dungeon 3.0**.
2. Progress through the Demon Lord X bosses until **🌊 Leviamon X**.
3. Defeat Leviamon X and farm the materials needed for its unlock progression.

> IMD 3.0 includes Beelzemon X → Demon X → Barbamon X → Lilithmon X → Belphemon
> Rage Mode → Leviamon X → Lucemon X.

## Unlock Leviamon X 🔓

Use the required Leviamon X materials through the DMW unlock system to obtain
**🌊 X-Antibody Factor Leviamon** — use it on your Leviamon to unlock Leviamon X.

## Important 💡

- Leviamon X is part of the **IMD 3.0** progression.
- IMD 3.0 gives **3 daily entry tickets** from **Birdramon** at DMW Village.
- Leviamon X requires an **XAI**.
- Attribute: **Virus** — Element: **Water**.

## Result ✅

**Leviamon** → ➡️ 🌊 **Leviamon X Unlocked**
