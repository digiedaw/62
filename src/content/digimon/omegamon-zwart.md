## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Omegamon Zwart is Virus / Dark / Jogress, unlocked from
**BlackWarGreymon + BlackMetalGarurumon** (Agumon Black + Gabumon Black sides).

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 70+ Tamer
- Lv. 70+ Agumon Black
- Lv. 70+ Gabumon Black
- Required Royal Knights / Omegamon progression

## Agumon Black Questline 🔥

1. Travel to **Yokohama Village** and speak with **Akihiro Kurata** to begin the
   Omegamon Zwart quest.
2. Travel to **Lost Historic Site** and defeat **20× Dokugumon**.
3. Continue in **Lost Historic Site** and defeat the required **BlackWarGreymon**.
4. Travel to **Waterfront** and defeat **20× Crowmon**.
5. Return to Kurata in **Yokohama Village**.
6. Continue to **Wind Valley** and complete the required **Murmukusmon**
   objective.

**✅ Agumon Black Side Complete**

## Gabumon Black Questline 🖤

1. Continue the **Gabumon Black** branch from the Omegamon Zwart questline.
2. Obtain the required summon card/item for the Gabumon Black progression.
3. Travel to **D-Terminal Underground Summon Square**, use the item and defeat
   the summoned **Omegamon** encounter.
4. Complete the remaining Gabumon Black objectives and return to the NPCs.

**✅ Gabumon Black Side Complete**

## Final Jogress 🔓

Complete both branches, then continue the **Omegamon Zwart Jogress** progression
to unlock:

> 🎁 **Omegamon Zwart**

## Important 💡

- Both partners must be **Lv. 70+**.
- The questline includes a **summoned Omegamon** encounter.
- Follow each partner branch in full before attempting the final Jogress unlock.

## Result ✅

**BlackWarGreymon + BlackMetalGarurumon** → ➡️ 🖤 **Omegamon Zwart Unlocked**
