---## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Alphamon Ouryuken X is unlocked from
**Alphamon X + OuRyumon X** (Dorumon + Ryudamon sides).
See also the [Alphamon Ouryuken X](/digimon/alphamon-ouryuken-x/) entry.

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 99+ Tamer
- Lv. 99+ Dorumon
- Alphamon X unlocked

> 📍 Pick up **"Efflorescences of Power"** from **Calumon** at **DATS Center**.
>
> ⚠️ Keep **Dorumon** as your Partner Digimon when accepting the quests.

## Dorumon Quest 🟣

1. Talk to **Dorumon \** at D-Terminal → then
   **Wizardmon \** at Versandi Terminal.
2. Defeat:
   - 30× **FanBeemon \**
   - 50× **Dracmon \**
3. Collect:
   - 15× **GranDracmon Low Class Instruction** — Dracmon
   - 5× **Mid Class Instruction** — Sangloupmon
   - 3× **High Class Instruction** — Matadormon
4. Defeat 5× **GranDracmon \**.
5. Collect **50× Unstable X-Antibody** from **DexDoruGreymon**.
6. Defeat **Examon \** Raid Boss.
7. Defeat **Omegamon \** Raid Boss.
8. Return to **Calumon**, then talk to **Omegamon \** at DATS Center.

**✅ Dorumon Side Complete**

## Ryudamon Side 🔵

There is no questline. You need:

- 🔹 **Alphamon Ouryuken X-Antibody Factor – Beta**

Use it to unlock the **Ryudamon** side. The **Beta** item is obtained through
**Events** or limited-time **Cash Shop** availability.

## Important 💡

- 🟣 **Alpha Factor** = Dorumon quest side.
- 🔵 **Beta Factor** = Ryudamon side.
- Requires **XAI + Jogress Chip** to Digivolve.

**Alphamon X + OuRyumon X** → ➡️ 🟣 **Alphamon Ouryuken X Unlocked**
