## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Boltboutamon is unlocked from **Myotismon** (PicoDevimon line)
through a long quest that culminates in **two Boltboutamon Duel Sphere**
encounters.

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 70+ Tamer
- Lv. 100+ PicoDevimon — Myotismon line

## Questline 🔥

1. Start the quest at **Richard Sampson** in **DATS Center**.
2. Travel to **Yokohama Village** and speak with **Sarah Damon**.
3. Go to **Yokohama Elementary School**, use the DigiVice and select **Report**,
   then return to Richard Sampson.
4. Travel to **Valley of Light** and speak with **Taichi**.
5. Defeat:
   - 20× Soulmon
   - 15× Soulmon Army Captain
   - 10× Soulmon Captain
   - 1× Soulmon Colossal

   Return to Taichi.
6. Continue in **Minato** and defeat:
   - 20× Witchmon
   - 15× Witchmon Army Captain
   - 10× Witchmon Captain
7. Use the **Boltboutamon Duel Sphere** and defeat **Boltboutamon**.
8. Travel to **Shibuya** and speak with **Izzy**.
9. Defeat:
   - 15× BlackGatomon
   - 15× Roachmon
   - 15× Vegiemon
   - 15× Soulmon
   - 15× Sangloupmon
10. Use the **Boltboutamon Duel Sphere** again and defeat Boltboutamon.
11. Use the DigiVice option **Return** to continue the quest.
12. Return to **Richard Sampson**.
13. Travel to the required location and speak with **Nanomon**.
14. Return to **Calumon**.
15. Return to **Nanomon** to complete the final quest.

**✅ Questline Complete**

## Unlock 🔓

The questline rewards:

> 🎁 **Boltboutamon Data Code — Myotismon**

Use it to unlock **Boltboutamon**.

## Alternative Requirement ➕

The **Piedmon** version of the Data Code is obtained through limited / Cash Shop
sources rather than the normal questline.

## Important 💡

- Tamer **Lv. 70+** required.
- **PicoDevimon — Myotismon** must be **Lv. 100+**.
- The questline contains **two Boltboutamon Duel Sphere** encounters.
- The DigiVice **Report** and **Return** functions are required during the quest.

## Result ✅

**Myotismon** → ➡️ ⚡ **Boltboutamon Unlocked**
