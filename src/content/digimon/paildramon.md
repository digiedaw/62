## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Paildramon is Vaccine / Free / Jogress, unlocked from
**Stingmon + ExVeemon** (Wormmon + Veemon sides).

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 70+ Tamer
- Lv. 31+ Wormmon (Stingmon unlocked)
- Lv. 31+ Veemon (ExVeemon unlocked)
- 4× disposable Jogress Chips

> 📍 Start at **Calumon** in **DATS Center**.

## Wormmon Questline 🐛

1. Start at **Calumon — DATS Center**.
2. Travel to **Frozen Ground** and speak with **Hawkmon Patrol**.
3. Travel to **Snowstorm Village** and defeat **IceDevimon**.
4. Travel to **Distorted Data Village** and defeat **Apemon**.
5. Return to Calumon.
6. Continue the Wormmon progression and complete **Lighting Up**.

**✅ Wormmon Side Complete**

## Veemon Questline 💎

1. Start the Veemon-side quest at **Calumon — DATS Center**.
2. Travel to **Dark Tower** and speak with **Patamon Patrol**.
3. Travel to **Western Area Outskirts** and defeat **Archnemon**.
4. Travel to **Wilderness Area** and defeat **Okuwamon**.
5. Return to Calumon.
6. Continue the Veemon progression and complete **A Momentary Peace**.

**✅ Veemon Side Complete**

## Unlock 🔓

After completing both progression chains:

- **Wormmon → Stingmon**
- **Veemon → ExVeemon**

complete the required **Jogress** quest, use the required **Jogress Chips** and
unlock:

> 🎁 **Paildramon**

## Important 💡

- Tamer **Lv. 70+** required.
- Wormmon and Veemon must be **Lv. 31+**.
- **Stingmon** and **ExVeemon** must already be unlocked.
- Any Digimon can be used to defeat the required enemies.
- The progression requires **4 disposable Jogress Chips**.
- The final quest is **not a daily quest** despite displaying a blue quest icon.

## Result ✅

**Stingmon + ExVeemon** → ➡️ 🐲 **Paildramon Unlocked**
