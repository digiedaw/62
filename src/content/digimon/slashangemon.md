---## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — SlashAngemon is Vaccine / Steel / Jogress, unlocked from
**Shakkoumon** (Patamon + Armadillomon sides).

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 70+ Tamer
- Lv. 100+ Patamon
- Shakkoumon (Jogress) unlocked

> 📍 Pick up the quest from **Calumon \** at **DATS Center**.
>
> ⚠️ Digivolve into **Shakkoumon (Jogress)** for the quest to appear.

## SlashAngemon Quest (Patamon Side) ⚔️

1. Talk to **Shoutmon** at Village of the Beginning.
2. Defeat Digimon at Silver Lake and collect **30× Blade Data**.
3. Defeat **Mystimon** (Historic Site Guardian) at Lost Historic Site.
4. Talk to **Shoutmon X5** at Village of the Beginning.
5. Use **Complete Blade Data** at D-Terminal Underground Summon Square.
6. Summon and defeat **SlashAngemon**.
7. Return to **Calumon** at DATS Center.

> 🎁 Receive **Strength of Potestas - A**.

**✅ Patamon Side Complete**

## Armadillomon Side 🟤

There is no additional questline. You need:

- 🔸 **Strength of Potestas - B**

Use it to unlock SlashAngemon for **Armadillomon**. The DMW guide lists this item as
obtainable from a **Cash Loot Box** during certain events.

## Important 💡

- **Strength of Potestas - A** = Quest reward.
- **Strength of Potestas - B** = Armadillomon side.
- SlashAngemon is **Vaccine / Steel / Jogress**.

## Result ✅

**Shakkoumon** → ➡️ ⚔️ **SlashAngemon Unlocked**
