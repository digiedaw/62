## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — Death-X-mon Awaken is unlocked from the
**Death-X-mon / DexDorugoramon X line** and requires **XAI + Jogress Chip**.

## Requirements 📋

- Death-X-mon / DexDorugoramon X line
- 🔴 X-Antibody Factor: Death-X-mon (Awaken) - Alpha
- 🔵 X-Antibody Factor: Death-X-mon (Awaken) - Beta
- XAI
- Jogress Chip

## Alpha Factor 🔴

1. Clear **📍 Royal Base Hard**.
2. Collect **🔸 X-Antibody Protoform**.
3. Go to **📍 UlforceVeedramon FM — DATS Center** and craft the
   **🔴 X-Antibody Factor: Death-X-mon (Awaken) - Alpha**.

> The Alpha factor is **Character Bound**.

## Beta Factor 🔵

There is no permanent Beta questline. You need the
**🔵 X-Antibody Factor: Death-X-mon (Awaken) - Beta**, obtained separately from the
limited **📦 Mysterious X-Antibody Factor [Death-X-mon (Awaken)]** Cash Shop box.

## Final Craft 🔨

Take **🔴 Alpha Factor ➕ 🔵 Beta Factor** to
**📍 UlforceVeedramon FM — DATS Center** and combine them to obtain:

**☠️ X-Antibody Factor: Death-X-mon (Awaken)** — use it to unlock the evolution.

## Important 💡

- 🔴 **Alpha** = Crafted using X-Antibody Protoform.
- 🔵 **Beta** = Obtained separately.
- **X-Antibody Protoform** comes from Royal Base Hard.
- You must **combine Alpha + Beta** to create the final unlock item.
- Requires **XAI + Jogress Chip** to Digivolve.

## Result ✅

**DexDorugoramon X** → ➡️ ☠️ **Death-X-mon Awaken Unlocked**
