## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Alphamon Ouryuken is Vaccine / Steel / Jogress, unlocked from
**Alphamon + OuRyuumon** (Dorumon + Ryuudamon sides).

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 55+ Tamer
- Lv. 41+ Dorumon (ReptileDramon line, Alphamon unlocked)
- Lv. 41+ Ryuudamon (OuRyuumon unlocked)

> 📍 Pick up the quest from **Calumon** at **DATS Center**.

## Dorumon Questline 🟣

1. Go to **Calumon** in DATS Center and begin the Alphamon Ouryuken Jogress
   quest. Follow the **Dorumon** branch.
2. Travel to **Infinite Ice Wall** and speak with **Gallantmon — Royal Knights**.
3. Defeat **Arkadimon (Mega)** to obtain **Gallantmon's Records**.
4. Return to Gallantmon and continue the investigation.
5. Travel to the required location and speak with **Leomon — X-Antibodies Predator**.
6. Defeat **10× Anubismon**, then return to the required NPC.
7. Receive **Transmission Terminal Gate A** from Calumon.

### DexDorugoramon Gate Chain 🌀

The gate progression runs **Gate A → Gate B → Gate C → Gate D**:

- **Gate A** — Use it around **Western Area Outskirts / Gloomy Outer Area** to
  summon **DexDorugoramon A**.
- **Gate B** — DexDorugoramon A always drops **Death-X Antibody A**, plus a
  chance to drop the next Gate. If Gate B doesn't drop, repeat Gate A.
- **Gate C** — Use Gate B to summon the next encounter and continue the chain.
- **Gate D** — Repeat until **Death-X Antibody D** is obtained.

> ⚠️ The **Death-X Antibody** items have guaranteed drops, but the next
> **Transmission Terminal Gate** is **not guaranteed** — higher gates summon
> stronger DexDorugoramon.
>
> 💡 **Quest owner should hit the summoned Digimon first.**

**✅ Dorumon Side Complete**

## Ryuudamon Questline 🔵

1. Return to **Calumon** and begin the **Ryuudamon** branch.
2. Travel to **Snowman Village** and continue the required Calumon objectives.
3. Defeat the required **Seadramon** to obtain **Legend of the Dragon King**.
4. Obtain the required **Aqua Orb** and use the detection stone near the
   penguins to continue the investigation.
5. Travel to the required location and defeat **DexDoruGreymon** to collect
   **200× Spoiled X Antibodies**.

**✅ Ryuudamon Side Complete**

## Final Jogress 🔓

After both branches are complete, continue with **Omegamon** for the remaining
**Jogress Chips** and unlock:

> 🎁 **Alphamon Ouryuken**

## Important 💡

- Dorumon must have the required **Alphamon** progression.
- Ryuudamon must have the required **OuRyuumon** progression.
- The DexDorugoramon gate chain can require multiple attempts because the next
  gate drop is RNG-dependent.
- **Death-X Antibody** drops themselves are guaranteed.

## Result ✅

**Alphamon + OuRyuumon** → ➡️ 🦖 **Alphamon Ouryuken Unlocked**
