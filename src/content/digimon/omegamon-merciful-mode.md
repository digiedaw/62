## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — Omegamon Merciful Mode is unlocked from **Omegamon** via the
crafted **📦 Omegamon Merciful Mode Evolution Item Box**.

## Requirements 📋

- Omegamon unlocked
- 1× DigiVice — Lost Light
- 2× Photon Spread
- 6 Digimon Lights (one each from the sacrificed Lv.120 Digimon)
- 100T

## DigiVice — Lost Light 🔷

Obtain **📦 DigiVice — Lost Light** from the limited **Mysterious Shiny Data Box**.

## Photon Spread ✨

Obtain **2× Photon Spread** from:

> 📍 **Odaiba Park Dungeon**

## Digimon Lights — Sacrifices ⚠️

Sacrifice the following **Lv.120 Digimon** at the **Omegamon NPC — DATS Center**
(near Kurata) to obtain their matching Digimon Light:

| Sacrificed Digimon | Material |
|---|---|
| Salamon Lv.120 | Digimon Light — HolyDramon |
| Patamon Lv.120 | Digimon Light — Seraphimon |
| Tentomon Lv.120 | Digimon Light — HerculesKabuterimon |
| Biyomon Lv.120 | Digimon Light — Phoenixmon |
| Gomamon Lv.120 | Digimon Light — Vikemon |
| Palmon [Original] Lv.120 | Digimon Light — Rosemon |

> ⚠️ Each sacrificed Digimon is **permanently deleted**.

## Final Craft 🔨

Take everything to **📍 UlforceVeedramon Future Mode — DATS Center** and craft:

**📦 Omegamon Merciful Mode Evolution Item Box**

### Materials

- 1× DigiVice — Lost Light
- 2× Photon Spread
- 1× Digimon Light — HolyDramon
- 1× Digimon Light — Seraphimon
- 1× Digimon Light — HerculesKabuterimon
- 1× Digimon Light — Phoenixmon
- 1× Digimon Light — Vikemon
- 1× Digimon Light — Rosemon

## Important 💡

- **6 Lv.120 Digimon** are required for the 6 Digimon Lights.
- All sacrificed Digimon are **permanently deleted**.
- **Photon Spread** comes from **Odaiba Park Dungeon**.
- **DigiVice — Lost Light** comes from the limited **Mysterious Shiny Data Box**.
- The completed evolution item is **Character Bound**.

## Result ✅

**Omegamon** → ➡️ ✨ **Omegamon Merciful Mode Unlocked**
