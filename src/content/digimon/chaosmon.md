## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Chaosmon does **not** have a normal NPC questline. It is unlocked
purely through **two Bug Data items** from event / loot-box sources.

## Requirements 📋

- 🐛 **Bug Data — Bancholeomon**
- 🐛 **Bug Data — Darkdramon**

## How to Unlock 🔓

1. Obtain the required **Bug Data** for:
   - **Bancholeomon**
   - **Darkdramon**
2. Use both required Bug Data items to unlock **Chaosmon**.

> ⚠️ There is no standard NPC questline for Chaosmon.
> 💡 The Bug Data is obtained through **event-related sources** and is
> **tradeable**.

## Important 💡

- No NPC questline exists for Chaosmon.
- Both Bug Data items are obtained through **event-related sources**.
- Both items are **tradeable**.

## Result ✅

**Bancholeomon + Darkdramon Bug Data** → ➡️ 🌀 **Chaosmon Unlocked**
