## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — Omegamon Zwart D evolves from **Omegamon Zwart** and requires
**both** unlock items.

## Requirements 📋

- Omegamon Zwart unlocked
- 🔴 D. Antivirus A
- ⚫ D. Antivirus B

## D. Antivirus A 🔴

1. Enter **Berserk Arena**.
2. Clear the dungeon and collect **🔸 30× Berserk Core**.
3. Go to **📍 UlforceVeedramon FM — DATS Center** and craft with
   **30× Berserk Core + 50T**.

> 🎁 **D. Antivirus A**

**✅ First Side Complete**

## D. Antivirus B ⚫

There is no permanent questline for this item in the base unlock guide. You need
**⚫ D. Antivirus B**, obtainable from **📦 Mysterious Data [Omegamon Zwart D]** —
a limited Cash Shop gamble box.

## Important 💡

- 🔴 **D. Antivirus A** = Crafted.
- ⚫ **D. Antivirus B** = Obtained separately.
- D. Antivirus A is **Character Bound**.
- **Berserk Core** is the reward material from Berserk Arena.
- DMW has also run **events** where D. Antivirus B could be crafted through event
  farming, so watch for future event alternatives.
