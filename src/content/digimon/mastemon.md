## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Mastemon is Vaccine / Light / Jogress, unlocked from
**Angewomon + LadyDevimon** (Gatomon + BlackGatomon sides).

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 90+ Tamer
- Lv. 100+ Salamon / Gatomon line
- Angewomon unlocked
- 🔸 Holy Amulet (quest reward)
- 🔹 Dark Amulet (BlackGatomon side)

> 📍 Pick up the quest from **Calumon** at **DATS Center** with your
> **Angewomon** as the partner Digimon.

## Angewomon Quest 😇

1. Talk to **White-bearded Guru** at DATS Center and begin the progression.
2. Obtain the **MagnaAngemon Summon Card** → use it at the **Duel Sphere** and
   defeat the summoned encounter.
3. Repeat for **Ophanimon → Cherubimon → Seraphimon** Summon Cards.

> 🎁 Receive **🔸 Holy Amulet** — use it on the Gatomon side to continue.

> ⚠️ If a summon timer expires, abandon and retake the quest before retrying.

**✅ Angewomon Side Complete**

## BlackGatomon Side 🖤

There is no permanent questline. You need:

- 🔹 **Dark Amulet**

The **Dark Amulet** is obtained through limited **event / Cash Shop** sources.

## Important 💡

- 🔸 **Holy Amulet** = Angewomon quest reward.
- 🔹 **Dark Amulet** = BlackGatomon side.
- Requires **Jogress Chip + Digiaura** to Digivolve.
- Make sure **Angewomon** is your partner Digimon when accepting the quest.

## Result ✅

**Angewomon + LadyDevimon** → ➡️ 😇 **Mastemon Unlocked**
