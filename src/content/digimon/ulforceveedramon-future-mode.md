## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — UlforceVeedramon Future Mode is unlocked from
**UlforceVeedramon** (V-mon line).

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 70+ Tamer
- Lv. 100+ V-mon (UlforceVeedramon Line)
- UlforceVeedramon unlocked

> 📍 Pick up the quest from **Jijimon** at **DMW Village**.

## Main Quest 🔵

1. Talk to **Taichi** at Western Village → then **Zeromaru** at Western Area: East.
2. Defeat **Greymon (Giant)** at Western Area: East.
3. Go to **Wind Valley** → Talk to **Gabou** and collect **5× Bandage**.
4. Continue to **MagnaAngemon** at Ruined Historic.
5. Enter **Duel Sphere** → Summon and defeat **ShogunGekomon**.
6. Defeat **DarkTyrannomon + Deltamon** at Wilderness Area.
7. Continue the questline until you return to **Jijimon** at Western Village.
8. Enter **Last Battle** at Western Village and defeat:
   - Arkadimon (Mega)
   - Daemon

**✅ Main Quest Complete**

## Into the Future ⚔️

After the main quest, complete:

1. Defeat:
   - 15× **Vajramon** — Vajramon Dungeon (Hard)
   - 15× **Vikaralamon** — Vikaralamon Dungeon (Hard)
2. Defeat:
   - 10× **Gankoomon X**
   - 10× **Jesmon X**
   - 10× **Examon X**

   > 📍 **Royal Base Hard**
3. Collect:
   - 🔹 **500× Digimental** — these drop from Digimon in the **Digital Area**.

## Final Craft 🔨

After completing everything, use the obtained materials to craft:

- 🔷 **Digimental - Code F**

at:

> 📍 **UlforceVeedramon Future Mode NPC — DATS Center**

Use **Digimental - Code F** to unlock Future Mode.

## Important 💡

- Complete **Into the Future #1 & #2** before the 500× Digimental quest appears.
- **Digimental** drops from Digimon throughout the **Digital Area**.
- If you later delete the Digimon/item, a quest from **Calumon** at DATS Center
  allows you to regain **Digimental - Code F**.

## Result ✅

**UlforceVeedramon** → ➡️ 🔵 **UlforceVeedramon Future Mode Unlocked**
