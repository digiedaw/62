## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — Moon Millenniummon is the evolved form of Millenniummon, unlocked
through an additional quest on the **Gazimon + BlackAgumon** partners.

## Requirements 📋

- Millenniummon unlocked
- Lv. 70+ Gazimon
- Lv. 70+ BlackAgumon
- Both partners on the Millenniummon evolution line

## Gazimon Questline 👹

1. Travel to **Dark Tower Wasteland** and speak with **Digimon Kaiser**.
2. Travel to **Silent Forest** and defeat **30× Bakemon**.
3. Continue in **Silent Forest** and defeat **10× Monodramon**.
4. Travel to **Distorted Data Village** and defeat **5× Millenniummon**.
5. Return to **Calumon** and complete the Gazimon branch.

**✅ Gazimon Side Complete**

## BlackAgumon Questline 🖤

1. Travel to **Dark Tower Wasteland** and speak with **Akihiro Kurata**.
2. Travel to **Digimon Maze Entrance** and defeat **30× Cerberumon**.
3. Return to **Dark Tower Wasteland** and defeat **10× Cherubimon**.
4. Travel to **Distorted Data Village** and defeat **5× Millenniummon**.
5. Return to **Calumon** and complete the BlackAgumon branch.

**✅ BlackAgumon Side Complete**

## Important 💡

- Both partners must be **Lv. 70+**.
- The Gazimon branch uses **Bakemon + Monodramon** in Silent Forest.
- The BlackAgumon branch uses **Cerberumon + Cherubimon**.
- Both branches end with **Millenniummon** encounters at Distorted Data Village.
