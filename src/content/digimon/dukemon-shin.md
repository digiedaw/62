## Overview

**Agumon** is a Reptile Digimon with tough, sharp claws. Although still a Rookie,
it has the fearless spirit of a warrior and grows rapidly through battle.
In *Digimon Adventure*, Agumon is the partner of **Taichi "Tai" Kamiya**, bearer
of the Crest of Courage.

> Fun fact: Agumon's design is one of the most recognizable Digimon ever —
> it appears across games, anime seasons, and even as a playable fighter.

## Attacks

- **Pepper Breath (Baby Flame)** — spits a scorching fireball from its mouth.
- **Claw Attack (Sharp Claws)** — slashes with the hard claws on both hands.
- **Spirit Fire** — fires an explosive flame orb (game appearances).
