## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — WarGreymon (Shin) is unlocked from **WarGreymon** via the final
**✨ Shining Wings of Courage** item.

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 120 Agumon (Rookie)
- 🔥 Shining Light of Courage
- 🛡️ Brave Shield

> 📍 Pick up **"Lost Brave Shield"** from **Calumon** at **DATS Center**.

## Brave Shield Quest 🛡️

Defeat:

1. 5× VenomMyotismon — 📍 Venomous Vortex (Hard)
2. 5× MetalGarurumon — 📍 Path of Friendship
3. 5× WarGreymon — 📍 Path of Bravery

> 🎁 Complete the quest to receive **🛡️ Brave Shield**.

**✅ Quest Complete**

## Shining Light of Courage 🔥

You also need **🔥 Shining Light of Courage** — obtained separately from the
limited **📦 Mysterious Soul Data Box [WarGreymon]** Cash Shop box.

## Final Craft 🔨

Combine at **📍 UlforceVeedramon Future Mode — DATS Center**:

- 🔥 Shining Light of Courage
- ➕ 🛡️ Brave Shield
- ➕ 💰 30T

> 🎁 Receive **✨ Shining Wings of Courage**

## Important 💡

- 🛡️ **Brave Shield** = Quest reward.
- 🔥 **Shining Light of Courage** = Obtained separately.
- **Brave Shield** is **Character Bound**.
- **Shining Wings of Courage** is **Character Bound**.

## Result ✅

**WarGreymon** → ➡️ 🔥 **WarGreymon (Shin) Unlocked**
