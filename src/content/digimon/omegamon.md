## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Omegamon is Vaccine / Light / Jogress, unlocked from
**WarGreymon + MetalGarurumon** (Agumon + Gabumon sides).

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 70+ Tamer
- Lv. 41+ Agumon (WarGreymon unlocked)
- Lv. 41+ Gabumon (MetalGarurumon unlocked)
- Digimon Maze F3 — Rooms 2 & 4 unlocked
- Jogress Chips from both branches

> 💡 If another player handles Room 4, you only need **access to F3**.

## Agumon Quest 🔥

1. Go to **Calumon** at DATS Center and accept the **Omegamon Jogress** quest.
   Follow the **Agumon** branch.
2. Defeat the required **Keramon Leaders** in **D-Terminal B2**.
3. Travel to **Yokohama Village** and speak with **Akihiro Kurata**.
4. Return to **Calumon** and continue the chain.
5. Travel to **Digimon Maze F3 Room 2** and defeat the required
   **Infermon (Destroyer)**.
6. Obtain a **Diablomon Time Bomb** from the **Diablomon** encounter at
   **Infinite Ice Wall**.

> ⏱️ The Time Bomb can take anywhere from **20 minutes to 2 hours** depending on
> luck.

7. Use the Time Bomb to summon **Armagemon** at **Digimon Maze Entrance Ruins**.
   Defeat Armagemon.

> ⚠️ **The player who owns the quest should hit Armagemon first.**

### Armagemon Notes

- ~840,000 HP, **Unknown** attribute, **weak to Light**.
- Long fight — **Burst Mode Digimon** can solo it.
- Armagemon's skills **bypass Evasion**.

8. Continue the chain with **Omegamon** to obtain the **Agumon-side Jogress Chip**.

**✅ Agumon Side Complete**

## Gabumon Quest ❄️

1. Return to **Calumon** and follow the **Gabumon** branch.
2. Travel to **Snowman Village** and defeat the required **Seadramon Leader**.
3. Travel to **Digimon Maze F3 Room 4** and defeat the required
   **Infermon (Inferno)**.
4. Obtain the required **Diablomon Time Bomb** from **Infinite Ice Wall**.
5. Continue the remaining Gabumon objectives and return to the NPCs to receive
   the **Gabumon-side Jogress Chip**.

**✅ Gabumon Side Complete**

## Important 💡

- The two branches **can be done in either order** — both must be completed.
- The **Diablomon Time Bomb** is the main luck-based step.
- Make sure **WarGreymon** and **MetalGarurumon** are unlocked before starting.
- Quest owner must attack **summoned bosses first**.

## Result ✅

**WarGreymon + MetalGarurumon** → ➡️ ⚔️ **Omegamon Unlocked**
