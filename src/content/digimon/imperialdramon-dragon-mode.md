## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Imperialdramon Dragon Mode is Vaccine / Free / Jogress, unlocked
from **Paildramon** (Wormmon + Veemon sides).

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 70+ Tamer
- Lv. 46+ Wormmon (Paildramon unlocked)
- Lv. 46+ Veemon (Paildramon unlocked)

> 📍 Pick up the quest from **Calumon** at **DATS Center**.

## Wormmon Branch 🐛

1. Go to **Calumon** in DATS Center and begin the Imperialdramon Dragon Mode
   questline.
2. Travel to **DATS Center** and speak with **Richard Sampson**.
3. Return to **Calumon** and continue the progression.
4. Obtain **Qinglongmon's Assist — V-mon** and use it on the Wormmon partner to
   unlock the Dragon Mode progression.

**✅ Wormmon Side Complete**

## Veemon Branch 💎

1. Begin the Veemon branch through **Calumon**.
2. Speak with **Richard Sampson** in DATS Center.
3. Return to **Calumon**.
4. Obtain **Qinglongmon's Assist — Wormmon** and use it on the Veemon partner to
   unlock the Dragon Mode progression.

**✅ Veemon Side Complete**

## Unlock 🔓

Complete both partner branches and use the corresponding **Qinglongmon's Assist**
items to unlock:

> 🎁 **Imperialdramon Dragon Mode**

## Important 💡

- Tamer **Lv. 70+** required.
- Both partners must be **Lv. 46+**.
- **Paildramon** must already be unlocked.
- The final quests may display a **blue quest icon** but are **not daily quests**.

## Result ✅

**Paildramon** → ➡️ 🐲 **Imperialdramon Dragon Mode Unlocked**
