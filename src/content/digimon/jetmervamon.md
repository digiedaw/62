---## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Jet Mervamon is Virus / Steel / Jogress, unlocked from
**Mervamon + Sparrowmon**.

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 70+ Tamer
- Lv. 100+ Sparrowmon

> 📍 Pick up the quest from **Calumon \** at **DATS Center**.

## Sparrowmon Quest 🦅

1. Talk to **OmegaShoutmon** at D-Terminal.
2. Go to **Digimon Farm** and defeat:
   - 20× Gatomon
   - 10× Gatomon (Leader)
3. Go to **Shinjuku Western Area (Day)** and collect **10× required quest items**.
4. Return to **OmegaShoutmon** at D-Terminal.
5. Use the **Jet Mervamon Summon Card** at the **Monster Card Dungeon** through
   **Phantomon** at D-Terminal.
6. Defeat **Jet Mervamon**.
7. Return to **OmegaShoutmon** and complete the questline.

> 🎁 Receive **Xros Fusion Light - Alpha**.

**✅ Sparrowmon Side Complete**

## Mervamon Side 🐍

There is no additional questline. You need:

- 🔹 **Xros Fusion Light - Beta**

Use it to unlock the **Mervamon** side. The DMW guide lists **Beta** as obtainable
from a **Cash Loot Box** during certain events.

## Important 💡

- 🔸 **Xros Fusion Light - Alpha** = Quest reward.
- 🔹 **Xros Fusion Light - Beta** = Obtained separately.
- Jet Mervamon is **Virus / Steel / Jogress**.

## Result ✅

**Mervamon + Sparrowmon** → ➡️ ✈️ **Jet Mervamon Unlocked**
