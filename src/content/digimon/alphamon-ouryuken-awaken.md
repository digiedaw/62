## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — Alphamon Ouryuken (Awaken) is unlocked from
**Alphamon Ouryuken X** and requires **XAI + Jogress Chip**.

## Requirements 📋

- Alphamon Ouryuken X unlocked
- 🔴 X-Antibody Factor - Alphamon Ouryuken (Awaken) - Alpha
- 🔵 X-Antibody Factor - Alphamon Ouryuken (Awaken) - Beta
- XAI
- Jogress Chip

## Alpha Factor 🔴

1. Enter **📍 Royal Base Hard**.
2. Clear the dungeon and collect **🧬 15× X-Antibody Protoform**.
3. Go to **📍 UlforceVeedramon FM — DATS Center** and craft using
   **15× X-Antibody Protoform + 30T**.

> 🎁 **X-Antibody Factor - Alphamon Ouryuken (Awaken) - Alpha**

**✅ Alpha Complete**

## Beta Factor 🔵

There is no permanent Beta questline. You need the
**🔵 X-Antibody Factor - Alphamon Ouryuken (Awaken) - Beta** — obtained separately
from the limited **📦 Mysterious X-Antibody Factor [Alphamon Ouryuken (Awaken)]**
Cash Shop box when available.

## Important 💡

- 🔴 **Alpha** = Crafted from Royal Base Hard materials.
- 🔵 **Beta** = Obtained separately.
- **Alpha** is **Character Bound**.
- Requires **XAI + Jogress Chip**.
- DMW has also offered **event methods** for obtaining Beta, so special events may
  provide an alternative.
