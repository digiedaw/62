---## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — Omegamon Alter-B evolves directly from **Omegamon Alter-S**.

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 100+ BlackAgumon [BlitzGreymon]
- Lv. 100+ Gabumon [CresGarurumon]
- Omegamon Alter-S unlocked

> 📍 Pick up their quests from **Calumon \** at **DATS Center**.

## Main Quests 🖤

1. Defeat:
   - 20× Dukemon (Shinjuku Protector)
   - 20× MegaGalgomon (Shinjuku Protector)
   - 20× Sakuyamon (Shinjuku Protector)

   And obtain **20× Emblem of a Knight**.

   > 📍 Emblems come from **Alphamon Ouryuken X** in Colosseum Hero R13 and/or
   > Colosseum Extreme R4.

2. Obtain:
   - **100× Proof of Knowledge**
   - **200× Scattered Black Digitron**

   > 📍 Scattered Black Digitron drops from any mob at **Minato City**.

**✅ Quest Complete**

## Unlock Items 🔨

You need both:

- 🔴 **Black Digitron - Sample A** — craft using **30× Berserk Core + 50M**.
  > 📍 Berserk Core comes from **Berserk Arena**.
- ⚫ **Black Digitron - Sample B** — craft using **2× Undetermined Black Digitron +
  50M**.
  > 📍 Undetermined Black Digitron comes from Calumon's **"The Berserker"** quests.

> 📍 Craft both at **UlforceVeedramon FM NPC — DATS Center**.

## Important 💡

- You need **both** Sample A + Sample B.
- Both unlock items are **Character Bound**.
- Sample A requires farming **Berserk Arena**.
- Omegamon Alter-B evolves directly from **Omegamon Alter-S**.

## Result ✅

**Omegamon Alter-S** → ➡️ 🖤 **Omegamon Alter-B Unlocked**
