## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Omegamon Alter-S is unlocked from
**BlitzGreymon + CresGarurumon** (BlackAgumon + Gabumon Black sides).

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 70+ Tamer
- Lv. 41+ BlackAgumon (BlitzGreymon unlocked)
- Lv. 41+ Gabumon Black (CresGarurumon unlocked)

> 📍 Pick up the quest from **Calumon** at **DATS Center**.

## BlitzGreymon Branch ⚡

1. Go to **Calumon** in DATS Center and begin the Omegamon Alter-S questline.
2. Travel to **File Island — Waterfront** and complete the required
   **BlitzGreymon** objective.
3. Defeat **1× BlitzGreymon Fighter**.
4. Evolve **BlackAgumon** into **BlitzGreymon**, then return to **Calumon**.
5. Travel back to **File Island — Waterfront** and defeat **5× GrandisKuwagamon**.
6. Return to **Calumon**.

**✅ BlitzGreymon Side Complete**

## CresGarurumon Branch ❄️

1. Travel to **Infinite Ice Wall** and complete the required **CresGarurumon**
   quest.
2. Defeat **20× SaberLeomon**.
3. Evolve **Gabumon Black** into **CresGarurumon**, then return to **Calumon**.

**✅ CresGarurumon Side Complete**

## Omegamon Examiner 🛡️

1. Travel to **DATS Center** and speak with **Omegamon — Royal Knights**.
2. Obtain the **Examiner Card**.
3. Travel to **D-Terminal Underground Summon Square** and use the card.
4. Summon and defeat **Omegamon Examiner**.
5. Return to **Calumon**.

## Unlock 🔓

Complete both partner branches and defeat Omegamon Examiner to receive:

> 🎁 **Jogress Data A — Omegamon Alter-S**

This unlocks **Omegamon Alter-S** for the **BlitzGreymon** partner.

## Additional Requirement ➕

The corresponding **Beta** evolution item is required for the other partner —
obtained through limited / event sources.

## Important 💡

- Both partners must meet the required level.
- **BlackAgumon** must have **BlitzGreymon** unlocked.
- **Gabumon Black** must have **CresGarurumon** unlocked.
- The **Omegamon Examiner** encounter takes place in
  **D-Terminal Underground Summon Square**.

## Result ✅

**BlitzGreymon + CresGarurumon** → ➡️ 🖤 **Omegamon Alter-S Unlocked**
