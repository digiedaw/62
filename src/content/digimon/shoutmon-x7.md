## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — Shoutmon X7 is unlocked from **Shoutmon DX + Xros Components**.

## Requirements 📋

All must be **Lv.70+** with **Shoutmon X5 unlocked**:

- Shoutmon (also needs OmegaShoutmon)
- Ballistamon
- Dorulumon
- Starmons
- Sparrowmon

> 📍 Pick up their quests from **Calumon** at **DATS Center** with the respective
> Digimon as your partner.

## Main Quest ⚔️

1. Go to **Western Area: East** and follow the NPC quests.
2. Defeat:
   - 50× DexDorugamon
   - 50× DexDorugamon (Leader)
   - 15× Deputymon
   - 15× Deputymon (Leader)
3. Collect:
   - 15× Cutemon's Trace from **Dobermon**
   - 15× Rockmon's Rock Fragment from **Rockmon**
4. In the **Monster Card Dungeon**, summon & defeat:
   Ballistamon → Dorulumon → Starmons → Sparrowmon
5. Return and continue the quests, then summon & defeat **ZekeGreymon**.
6. Continue again and summon & defeat **OmegaShoutmon + Shoutmon DX**.
7. Finally defeat in **GreenZone**:
   - 200× Apemon
   - 200× Flymon
   - 200× Meramon
   - 200× Icemon

**✅ Main Quest Complete**

## Burning Rocker 🔥

After the questline you unlock a **Daily Quest**. Sacrifice Lv.120:

- 10× Shoutmon
- 10× Greymon (C)
- 10× MailBirdramon

Each set of Souls completes the Daily once and gives **🔥 1× Burning Rocker**.

You need **10× Burning Rocker**.

## Final Craft 🔨

You need:

- 15× Divine Aura
- 10× Burning Rocker
- 100T

> 📍 Craft at **UlforceVeedramon FM — DATS Center**.

> 🎁 Receive **Xros Evolution Item Box [Shoutmon X7]** containing:
> 🔴 **Great Xros - Alpha** + 🔵 **Great Xros - Beta**

You also need **👑 50× Code Crown**.

## Important 💡

- **Divine Aura** = clear reward from **Bagramon's Domain**.
- You need **30 Lv.120 Digimon sacrifices** total.
- Sacrificed Digimon are **permanently deleted**.
- **Great Xros Alpha + Beta + 50 Code Crown** are all required to unlock X7.

## Result ✅

**Shoutmon DX + Xros Components** → ➡️ 🔥 **Shoutmon X7 Unlocked**
