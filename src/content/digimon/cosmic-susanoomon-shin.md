## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — CosmicSusanoomon (Shin) is Vaccine / Pitch Black / Jogress and a
**Tank-type** Digimon, evolving from **Cosmic Susanoomon**.

## Requirements 📋

- Cosmic Susanoomon
- 🛡️ Overseer of Protection
- ✨ Overseer of Restoration

## Overseer of Protection 🛡️

Obtain **🛡️ Overseer of Protection** — use it to unlock the **first side** of
CosmicSusanoomon (Shin).

## Overseer of Restoration ✨

Obtain **✨ Overseer of Restoration** — use it to unlock the **second side**.

**Both unlock items are required** for the complete evolution.

## Important 💡

- 🛡️ **Overseer of Protection** = First unlock item.
- ✨ **Overseer of Restoration** = Second unlock item.
- Evolves from **Cosmic Susanoomon**.
- CosmicSusanoomon (Shin) is **Vaccine / Pitch Black / Jogress** — **Tank type**.
