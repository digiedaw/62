## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Millenniummon is Virus / Dark / Jogress, unlocked from
**Matadormon + Chaosdramon** (Gazimon + BlackAgumon sides).

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 70+ Tamer (first unlock)
- Lv. 60+ Tamer (re-hatching)
- Gazimon on the Millenniummon line
- BlackAgumon on the Millenniummon line

> 💡 After unlocking Millenniummon once, the Tamer level requirement drops to
> **Lv. 60+** for subsequent hatches.

## Gazimon Questline 👹

1. Travel to **Dark Tower Wasteland** and speak with **Digimon Kaiser**.
2. Travel to **Ruined Historic** and defeat **30× Megadramon**.
3. Travel to **Infinite Mountain** and defeat the required **Millenniummon**
   encounter (located near **Mushroomon**).
4. Return to **Calumon** and complete the Gazimon branch.

**✅ Gazimon Side Complete**

## BlackAgumon Questline 🖤

1. Travel to **Snowman Village** and speak with **Akihiro Kurata**.
2. Travel to **Infinite Ice Wall** and defeat **30× SkullGreymon**.
3. Travel to **Infinite Mountain** and defeat the required **Millenniummon**
   encounter (located near the Infinite Mountain Dungeon entrance).
4. Return to **Calumon** and complete the BlackAgumon branch.

**✅ BlackAgumon Side Complete**

## Important 💡

- First unlock requires **Tamer Lv. 70+**.
- Re-hatching requires **Tamer Lv. 60+**.
- Both partners must be on the **Millenniummon evolution line**.
- Each branch finishes with a **Millenniummon** encounter at Infinite Mountain.

## Result ✅

**Matadormon + Chaosdramon** → ➡️ 💀 **Millenniummon Unlocked**
