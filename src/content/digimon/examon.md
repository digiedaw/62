## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Examon is unlocked from **Slayerdramon + Breakdramon**
(Dracomon Green + Dracomon Blue sides).

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 60+ Tamer
- Lv. 41+ Dracomon Green (Breakdramon unlocked)
- Lv. 41+ Dracomon Blue (Slayerdramon unlocked)
- Emperor's Wing for the Blue Dracomon slot

## Dracomon Green Branch 🟢

1. Go to **Calumon — Jogress** in DATS Center and begin the Examon questline.
2. Talk to **Calumon** and begin the required investigation.
3. Defeat the required **SkullGreymon** until the quest item is obtained.
   Return to Calumon.
4. Calumon provides **4× Coil Data Hack** on:
   - MetalGarurumon — Distorted Data Village
   - Vikemon — Distorted Data Village
   - Volcamon — Oil Refinery-2
   - Guardromon — Oil Refinery-2

   Return to Calumon after using them.
5. Receive the **Slayerdramon Summon Card** → travel to **Summon Square** and use
   it. Defeat the summoned **Slayerdramon** and return to Calumon.
6. Continue the questline and collect **Data Access Coil** in:
   - Oil Refinery-2
   - Oil Refinery-3
7. Travel to **Oil Refinery-3** and use the Data Access Coil to summon
   **Machinedramon**. Defeat Machinedramon.
8. Return to the required NPCs and complete the remaining **Jogress Chip**
   quests.

**✅ Dracomon Green Side Complete**

## Dracomon Blue Branch 🔵

The Blue Dracomon branch requires the corresponding **Slayerdramon** progression
and the required slot-unlock item:

### Emperor's Wing 🪶

The **Emperor's Wing** is required to unlock the **Blue Dracomon** slot.

The item is obtained through **events** or limited **Cash Shop** availability.

> ⚠️ Although the Emperor's Wing is required for the Blue Dracomon slot, the
> associated quest progression is still required for the overall Examon unlock.

## Unlock 🔓

Complete:

- ✅ Dracomon Green questline
- ✅ Slayerdramon progression
- ✅ Machinedramon encounter
- ✅ Dracomon Blue requirements (incl. Emperor's Wing)
- ✅ Required Jogress Chips

After all of these, **Examon** can be unlocked.

## Important 💡

- Both **Dracomon** partners are required.
- The **Green branch** contains the main quest progression.
- The **Emperor's Wing** is a limited / event-related requirement.
- The **Slayerdramon Summon Card** is used at the **Summon Square**.

## Result ✅

**Slayerdramon + Breakdramon** → ➡️ 🐉 **Examon Unlocked**
