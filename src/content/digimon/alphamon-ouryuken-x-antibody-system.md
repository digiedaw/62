## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Alphamon Ouryuken (X-Antibody System) is unlocked
through Dorumon's **Royal Knights** questline. Ryudamon uses a
separate progression path.

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 99+ Tamer
- Lv. 99+ **Dorumon** (must remain partner throughout)
- Alphamon X-Antibody System item

## Quest Progression 🗺️

1. Start the **Efflorescences of Power** progression at **Calumon**.
2. Speak with **Dorumon** in **D-Terminal**.
3. Continue through the **Wizardmon Resistance / Versandi** progression.
4. Defeat the required **Fanbeemon** in Evolved.
5. Defeat the required **Dracmon** in Evolved.
6. Collect the required **GranDracmon Instructions**.
7. Defeat the required **GranDracmon**.
8. Collect **Unstable X-Antibody** from **DexDoruGreymon**.
9. Complete the **Examon Royal Knights** raid.
10. Complete the **Omegamon Royal Knights** raid.
11. Return to **Calumon**.
12. Continue the final **Royal Knights** progression.

> 💡 **Ryudamon** does NOT require this questline — it uses the
> appropriate **Alphamon Ouryuken X-Antibody Factor** instead.

## Notes

- ⚠️ Dorumon **must remain the partner Digimon** throughout the required
  quest progression. Switching partner resets progress.
- See also: [Alphamon Ouryuken X](/digimon/alphamon-ouryuken-x/) for the
  base variant.
