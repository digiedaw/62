## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — Lucemon X is Virus / Pitch Black / Burst Mode X, unlocked from
**Lucemon** via its X-Antibody Factors.

## Requirements 📋

- Lucemon
- Lucemon X-Antibody Factor - Alpha
- Lucemon X-Antibody Factor - Beta
- XAI

## Alpha Factor 🔴

1. Enter **📍 Infinite Mountain Dungeon 3.0**.
2. Clear the dungeon and defeat the Demon Lord X bosses leading up to
   **😈 Lucemon X**.

   > IMD 3.0 contains Beelzemon X, Demon X, Barbamon X, Lilithmon X, Belphemon
   > Rage Mode, Leviamon X and Lucemon X.

3. Farm the required Lucemon X crafting material from IMD 3.0.
4. Collect **🔸 20× Required Material**.

   > 💡 This was reduced from 30 → 20 in the **August 2025 update**.

5. Use the materials to craft **🔴 Lucemon X-Antibody Factor - Alpha**.

## Beta Factor 🔵

You also need **🔵 Lucemon X-Antibody Factor - Beta** — obtained separately through
the **📦 Mysterious X-Antibody Factor Box [Lucemon X]** when available in the Cash
Shop.

## Important 💡

- 🔴 **Alpha** = Farmed/crafted through IMD 3.0 progression.
- 🔵 **Beta** = Obtained separately.
- IMD 3.0 gives **3 daily entry tickets** from **Birdramon** at DMW Village.
- Lucemon X requires an **XAI** to Digivolve.
- Lucemon X is **Virus / Pitch Black / Burst Mode X**.

## Result ✅

**Lucemon** → ➡️ 😈 **Lucemon X Unlocked**
