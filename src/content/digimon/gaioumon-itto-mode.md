## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — Gaioumon Itto Mode is Virus / Steel / Burst Mode X, unlocked from
**Gaioumon**.

## Requirements 📋

- Gaioumon
- 1× Soul of a Warrior
- 10× Oni Data
- 30T

> 📍 Final crafting is done at **UlforceVeedramon FM — DATS Center**.

## Oni Data 👹

1. Enter **📍 Royal Base Hard**.
2. Clear the dungeon to obtain **👹 Oni Data**.

You need **10× Oni Data**.

**✅ Oni Data Complete**

## Soul of a Warrior ⚔️

You also need **🗡️ 1× Soul of a Warrior** — obtained separately from the limited
**📦 Mysterious X-Antibody Factor [Gaioumon Itto Mode]** Cash Shop box.

## Final Craft 🔨

Take **1× Soul of a Warrior + 10× Oni Data + 30T** to
**📍 UlforceVeedramon FM — DATS Center** and craft:

**X-Antibody Factor: Gaioumon Itto Mode**

## Important 💡

- 👹 **Oni Data** = Royal Base Hard clear reward.
- 🗡️ **Soul of a Warrior** = Obtained separately.
- **Oni Data** and the final **X-Antibody Factor** are **Character Bound**.
- Gaioumon Itto Mode is **Virus / Steel / Burst Mode X**.

## Result ✅

**Gaioumon** → ➡️ 🗡️ **Gaioumon Itto Mode Unlocked**
