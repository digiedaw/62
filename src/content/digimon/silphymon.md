## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Silphymon is Vaccine / Wind / Jogress, unlocked from
**Aquilamon + Gatomon** (Hawkmon + Salamon sides).

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 55+ Tamer
- Lv. 70+ Salamon
- Lv. 70+ Hawkmon

> ⚠️ Only evolve the partners when they meet the required level.
> 💡 **Start the Hawkmon questline first** — this is important because it
> allows the final Gatomon quests to become available.

## Gatomon Branch 😺

1. Travel to **Silver Lake** and speak with **Gatomon** to begin the Silphymon
   questline.
2. Defeat the required **Elecmon** in Silver Lake and obtain the quest item.
   Return to Gatomon.
3. Travel to **Silver Lake** and speak with **Wormmon**.
4. Travel to **Silent Forest** and complete the required **Veemon** objective.
5. Travel to **Lost Historic Site** and complete the required **Hawkmon**
   objective.
6. Return to **Calumon** in DATS Center.
7. Travel to **Lost Historic Site** and defeat the required **Blossomon**.
   Obtain the required quest item and return to Calumon.

**✅ Gatomon Side Complete**

## Hawkmon Branch 🦅

1. Begin the Hawkmon branch through **Gatomon** at **Silver Lake**.
2. Defeat the required **Elecmon** in Silver Lake and obtain the quest item.
3. Travel to **Waterfront** and defeat the required **Archnemon**. Obtain the
   quest item and return to the required NPC.

**✅ Hawkmon Side Complete**

## Unlock 🔓

After completing both partner questlines:

- ✅ Salamon branch
- ✅ Hawkmon branch

the **Silphymon** Jogress evolution becomes available.

## Important 💡

- Tamer **Lv. 55+** required.
- Both partners must be **Lv. 70+**.
- **Hawkmon's quest should be started first**.
- The questline requires the relevant partner Digimon to be present for the
  appropriate progression.

## Result ✅

**Aquilamon + Gatomon** → ➡️ 🦋 **Silphymon Unlocked**
