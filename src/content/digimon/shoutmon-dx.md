## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Shoutmon DX is Jogressed from **OmegaShoutmon + ZekeGreymon**.

## Requirements 📋

- OmegaShoutmon unlocked
- ZekeGreymon unlocked
- 🔸 Double Fusion - DX - Alpha
- 🔹 Double Fusion - DX - Beta
- 10× Code Crown for the Shoutmon line

## Crusade Mode Quests ⚔️

> 📍 Talk to **Pickmon** at **D-Terminal**.

1. Defeat in **Green Zone (Advanced)**:
   - 1× OmegaShoutmon
   - 1× ZekeGreymon
2. Defeat in **Green Zone (Night) (Guild)**:
   - 1× Shoutmon X4
   - 1× Shoutmon X5
   - 1× Shoutmon DX
   - 1× Shoutmon X7

> 🎁 These quests reward **Gold Digizoid Data**.

## Craft Unlock Items 🔨

You need a total of **6× Gold Digizoid Data**:

- 3× Gold Digizoid Data → **Double Fusion - DX - Alpha**
- 3× Gold Digizoid Data → **Double Fusion - DX - Beta**

> 📍 Craft them at the **OmegaShoutmon NPC** in **D-Terminal**.

## Important 💡

- 🔸 **Alpha** = MailBirdramon side.
- 🔹 **Beta** = Greymon (C) side.
- **10× Code Crown** = Shoutmon side.
- All **3 evolution slots** must be unlocked to use Shoutmon DX.

## Result ✅

**OmegaShoutmon + ZekeGreymon** → ➡️ 🔥 **Shoutmon DX Unlocked**
