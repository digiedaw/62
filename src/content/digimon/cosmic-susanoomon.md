## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Cosmic Susanoomon is Jogressed directly from
**Lunar KaiserGreymon + Solar MagnaGarurumon**.

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 99+ Lunar KaiserGreymon
- Lv. 99+ Solar MagnaGarurumon
- Jogress Chip
- Spirit Accelerator

## Lunar KaiserGreymon 🌙

1. Raise the required **Human-type Spirit Digimon** to Lv. 120.
2. Exchange the Lv. 120 Spirits to obtain the required **Spirits of Lunar**.
3. Use them through the **Advanced Spirit System** to obtain:
   🌙 **Lunar KaiserGreymon**

## Solar MagnaGarurumon ☀️

1. Raise the required **Beast-type Spirit Digimon** to Lv. 120.
2. Exchange the Lv. 120 Spirits to obtain the required **Spirits of Solar**.
3. Use them through the **Advanced Spirit System** to obtain:
   ☀️ **Solar MagnaGarurumon**

> The DMW **Advanced Spirit System** uses Lv. 120 Spirit Digimon as the basis for
> creating the Lunar/Solar progression.

## Important 💡

- Both Lunar KaiserGreymon **and** Solar MagnaGarurumon are needed.
- They can Jogress into Cosmic Susanoomon from **Lv. 99**.
- You need both a **Jogress Chip** and a **Spirit Accelerator** to Digivolve.

## Result ✅

🌙 Lunar KaiserGreymon + ☀️ Solar MagnaGarurumon → ➡️ 🌌 **Cosmic Susanoomon Unlocked**
