## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — Susanoomon (Shin) is unlocked from **Susanoomon + Susanoomon**
(both sides need their own Overseer unlock item).

## Requirements 📋

- Susanoomon unlocked
- Spirit of the Lunar
- Spirit of the Solar
- Supreme Evolution Summon Card
- Cosmic Souls

> 📍 The required Warrior Core quests are obtained from **Calumon** at **DATS Center**.

## Overseer of Destruction 🌙

1. Obtain **Spirit of the Lunar** by exchanging a Lv.120 **Lunar KaiserGreymon**.
2. Obtain **Cosmic Souls** by defeating Digimon summoned with Supreme Evolution
   Summon Cards.
3. Complete **Calumon's Warrior Core** quest.
4. Craft **🔴 Overseer of Destruction** at **UlforceVeedramon Future Mode — DATS
   Center**.

## Overseer of Regeneration ☀️

1. Obtain **Spirit of the Solar** by exchanging a Lv.120 **Solar MagnaGarurumon**.
2. Obtain the required **Cosmic Souls**.
3. Complete **Calumon's Warrior Core** quest.
4. Craft **🔵 Overseer of Regeneration** at **UlforceVeedramon Future Mode — DATS
   Center**.

## Important 💡

- 🔴 **Overseer of Destruction** = one Susanoomon side.
- 🔵 **Overseer of Regeneration** = the other Susanoomon side.
- You need **both** unlock items.
- **Supreme Evolution Summon Card V1** can be obtained through **Colosseum Hero**,
  starting from Round 10.
- Requires a **Jogress Chip** to Digivolve.

## Result ✅

**Susanoomon + Susanoomon** → ➡️ ⚡ **Susanoomon (Shin) Unlocked**
