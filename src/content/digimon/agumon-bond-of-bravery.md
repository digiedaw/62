## Overview

**Rank: SSS+** — Agumon — Bond of Bravery is unlocked from **WarGreymon** via the
final **🔥 Last Evolution Courage** item.

## Requirements 📋

- Agumon / WarGreymon
- 🔴 Light of Courage - Alpha
- 🔵 Light of Courage - Beta
- Final unlock item: **🔥 Last Evolution Courage**

## Light of Courage - Alpha 🔴

1. Enter the **📍 Never-Land Dungeon**.
2. Clear the dungeon and obtain **🔸 3× DigiVice of Evolution**.
3. Go to **📍 UlforceVeedramon FM — DATS Center** and craft
   **🔴 Light of Courage - Alpha**.

## Light of Courage - Beta 🔵

There is no permanent questline for Beta. You need
**🔵 Light of Courage - Beta**, obtainable from the limited
**📦 Mysterious Crest [Agumon]** Cash Shop box.

## Final Craft 🔨

Combine at **📍 UlforceVeedramon FM — DATS Center**:

- 🔴 Light of Courage - Alpha
- ➕ 🔵 Light of Courage - Beta
- ➕ 💰 30T

> 🎁 Receive **🔥 Last Evolution Courage**

## Important 💡

- 🔴 **Alpha** = Crafted using 3× DigiVice of Evolution.
- 🔵 **Beta** = Obtained separately.
- **DigiVice of Evolution** comes from Never-Land.
- **Alpha** and **Last Evolution Courage** are **Character Bound**.

## Result ✅

**WarGreymon** → ➡️ 🔥 **Agumon — Bond of Bravery Unlocked**
