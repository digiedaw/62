## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Fanglongmon is Vaccine / Light / Jogress, unlocked by completing
all four **Holy Beast** branches.

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 40+ Tamer
- Lv. 41+ Qinglongmon (Mega unlocked)
- Lv. 41+ Baihumon (Mega unlocked)
- Lv. 41+ Zhuqiaomon (Mega unlocked)
- Lv. 41+ Xuanwumon (Mega unlocked)

> 📍 Start at **Calumon — Jogress** in **DATS Center**.
> 💡 Each Holy Beast has its own branch — **all four must be completed**.

## Qinglongmon Branch 🌿

1. Start the Qinglongmon branch through **Calumon — Jogress** in DATS Center.
2. Travel to **Snowman Village** and speak with **Qinglongmon**.
3. Defeat **Wendigomon** and collect **12× Qinglongmon's Digizoid**.
4. Travel to **Distorted Data Village** and defeat the required **Seraphimon**
   encounter.
5. Return to Calumon with the required item.

**✅ Qinglongmon Side Complete**

## Baihumon Branch 🐯

1. Travel to **Western Area — West** and speak with **Baihumon**.
2. Defeat **Okuwamon** and collect **12× Baihumon's Digizoid**.
3. Travel to **White Dark Tower Wasteland** and defeat the required
   **Cherubimon** encounter.
4. Return to Calumon.

**✅ Baihumon Side Complete**

## Zhuqiaomon Branch 🔥

1. Travel to **Oil Refinery-3** and speak with **Zhuqiaomon**.
2. Defeat **NeoDevimon** and collect **12× Zhuqiaomon's Digizoid**.
3. Travel to **Ruined Historic** and defeat the required **Ophanimon**
   encounter.
4. Return to Calumon.

**✅ Zhuqiaomon Side Complete**

## Xuanwumon Branch 💧

1. Travel to **Silver Lake** and speak with **Xuanwumon**.
2. Defeat **Elecmon** and collect **12× Xuanwumon's Digizoid**.
3. Travel to **Lost Historic Site** and defeat the required **Lucemon**
   encounter.
4. Return to Calumon.

**✅ Xuanwumon Side Complete**

## Final Unlock 🔓

After completing all four Holy Beast branches, continue the final Jogress
progression with **Calumon** and complete the required Jogress objectives to
unlock:

> 🎁 **Fanglongmon**

## Important 💡

- All four **Holy Beasts** are required.
- Each branch requires **12 Digizoid** from the specified Digimon.
- The four boss encounters are:
  - **Seraphimon**
  - **Cherubimon**
  - **Ophanimon**
  - **Lucemon**

## Result ✅

**Qinglongmon + Baihumon + Zhuqiaomon + Xuanwumon** → ➡️ 🐉 **Fanglongmon Unlocked**
