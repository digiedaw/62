## Overview

**Rank: SSS** — Valkyrimon is Vaccine / Wind / Jogress, unlocked from
**Silphymon** (Hawkmon + Salamon sides).

## Requirements 📋

- Lv. 70+ Tamer
- Lv. 70+ Hawkmon (Silphymon unlocked)
- Lv. 70+ Salamon (Silphymon unlocked)

## Questline ⚔️

1. Evolve the required partner into **Silphymon**.
2. Travel to **DATS Center** and speak with **Calumon** to begin the Valkyrimon
   progression.
3. Travel to **Frozen Ground** and continue the quest involving
   **Valkyrimon** and **Freyja**.
4. Travel to **Digimon Maze F2** and defeat **60× Grizzlymon**.

## Valkyrimon Branch 🟣

Choose the **Valkyrimon** progression.

1. Travel to **Western Area — East** and defeat:
   - 1× Panjyamon
   - 15× Shawujingmon
   - 20× Bastemon

   > ⚠️ Defeat **Panjyamon last**.
2. Travel to **Digimon Farm** and defeat:
   - 1× Astamon
   - 15× BlackWereGarurumon
   - 20× Wendigomon

   > ⚠️ Defeat **Astamon last**.
3. Travel to **Wind Valley** and defeat **1× ShadowSeraphimon**.

## Sealed Aurvandil 🔓

1. Return to **Valkyrimon** and continue the quest.
2. Obtain **Sealed Aurvandil**.
3. Return to **Calumon** and complete the objective to receive:
   - 🎁 **Aurvandil** (required for the Salamon side).

## Partner Requirements ➕

The Valkyrimon progression also requires the corresponding partner-side item:

- 🟣 **Aurvandil** — required for **Salamon**
- 🟢 **Fenrir** — required for **Hawkmon**

**Fenrir** is obtained through the corresponding limited / event item source.

## Unlock 🔓

Complete the Valkyrimon questline and obtain:

- 🟣 **Aurvandil**
- 🟢 **Fenrir**

Then complete the partner requirements to unlock:

> 🎁 **Valkyrimon**

## Important 💡

- Both partners must have **Silphymon** unlocked.
- Both partners must be **Lv. 70+**.
- The first **Silphymon** evolution is required before beginning the main
  progression.
- When a quest requires multiple monster types plus a boss, **defeat the named
  boss last** when specified.

## Result ✅

**Silphymon** → ➡️ 🪽 **Valkyrimon Unlocked**
