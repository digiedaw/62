## Overview

Dungeons are multi-floor instanced areas packed with wild Digimon, treasure and
boss encounters. This guide covers the basics every DigiDestined should know
before descending.

## Before you enter

- Stock up on healing items — floors get progressively harder
- Bring a balanced partner (at least one Vaccine and one Data type in the party)
- Save often; boss floors have no checkpoint

## Floor strategy

1. Clear side rooms first — they hide rare spawns and chests
2. Watch for trap tiles near dead ends
3. Retreat to a previous floor to heal if your party drops below half HP

> Tip: Some rare Digimon only appear on specific floors. Check the spawn table
> posted by the community before farming.
