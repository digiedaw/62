# New Player Progression Guide

A step-by-step guide for new players, covering the tutorial, Starter and Journey Digimon setup, early questing, seals, decks, Transcendence, and preparation for Early Game content.

---

## 1. Tutorial

You'll begin the game with an introductory fight as **Omegamon X against Dexmon**, showcasing the game's basic combat.

After the fight, the initial tutorial begins. Follow the tutorial quests until you are teleported to **Jijimon in DMW Village**.

### Important Items

Keep the following items. Do not use them immediately:

* Journey Exchange Ticket
* Starter Package Ticket

You'll use them after completing the tutorial.

> **Note:** Don't worry about the **"A New World"** quest yet. New players cannot complete the entire questline. You can return to it later.

The questline is still worth completing eventually because it unlocks **UlforceVeedramon Future Mode**.

---

## 2. City of Beginnings

After completing **"Welcome to the Digital World"**, accept the **City of Beginnings** questline.

Continue following the tutorial until **Teacher Mori** gives you **"A New Journey"** and you are teleported back to DMW Village in front of Elecmon.

### Items to Save

Besides the following expansion items:

* Inventory Expansion
* Warehouse Expansion
* Digimon Archive Expansion

it is generally better to save your tutorial items instead of immediately using them.

For example, the **1-Day Jogress Chip** isn't useful yet because you don't have a Digivice to equip it on.

### Items You Should Use

Use and equip:

* 1-Day DigiAura
* 1-Day Sneakers
* 1-Day DATS Uniform

Your base movement speed is quite slow, so these items make early questing much easier.

The DigiAura also makes the tutorial easier because you won't need to worry as much about DS costs for your Starter Digimon.

---

## 3. Prepare in DMW Village

Once you finish the City of Beginnings questline, you'll return to DMW Village in front of Elecmon.

Before accepting **"A New Journey II"**, prepare your character.

### Start the Novice Questline

Accept the quests from:

* Tsunomon
* Koromon

Continue these quests alongside your normal progression.

### Choose Your Starter Package

Trade your **Starter Exchange Ticket** for the package corresponding to your chosen Starter.

> **Important:** The package does not contain a Starter Egg. Choosing the wrong package will make progression more difficult.

### Choose Your Journey Digimon

Trade your **Journey Exchange Ticket** for the Journey Digimon corresponding to your class.

| Class  | Journey Digimon |
| ------ | --------------- |
| SK DPS | Dukemon Shin    |
| AA DPS | LordKnightmon X |

---

## 4. Starting Packages

You should now have three important packages:

1. Starter Package
2. Journey Exchange Package
3. Lala's Gift Package

> **Warning:** Don't open every package inside these packages immediately. Read ahead first.

### Starter Package

The Starter Package contains:

* SS+ Evolution Item
* SSS Evolution Item
* 15 Mega Reinforced DigiClone Set Boxes `[Savers]`
* 2 Level 5 Raremon Digi-Eggs
* 2 Mid Class Transcendence Chips
* 500 Fruit of Homeostasis `[Savers]`
* 1 Growth Fruit `[Savers]`
* 11 Jumping Crystal `[Digimon]`

### `[Savers]` DigiClones

`[Savers]` DigiClones can only be used on Starter Digimon:

* Agumon (S)
* Lalamon
* Falcomon
* Gaomon

They cannot be used on your Journey Digimon.

In simple terms:

> `[Savers]` DigiClones → Starter Digimon
> Normal DigiClones → Other Digimon

Open the Mega Reinforced DigiClone Sets and clone your Starter.

Keep the remaining Clones for later. After Transcending your Starter, use them on HP.

### Raremon Digi-Eggs

Don't hatch the two Level 5 Raremon Digi-Eggs yet.

You probably don't have enough Devil Hatch Data at this stage.

### Transcendence Items

Don't worry about Transcendence yet. You probably won't have enough money or materials.

The Growth Fruit `[Savers]` will increase your Starter to **125%**, allowing it to use the Transcendence system.

---

## 5. Journey Package

Your Journey Package contains:

* Lv. 5 DigiEgg for your chosen Journey Digimon
* SSS Evolution unlock items
* 50 Evoluters
* Class-specific Gift Package
* 10 Jumping Crystal `[Tamer]`
* 12 Jumping Crystal `[Digimon]`
* 50 Low Class Data
* Class-specific Underworld Accessory Box

### Evoluters

Most Digimon initially only have their Champion evolution unlocked.

Evoluters are used to unlock many additional evolutions.

Some higher-rank evolutions require unique evolution items instead.

### Class Gift Package

The package contains several useful items, including:

* 7-Day Tamer Skills
* Transcendence Fruits
* Mercenary Slot Expansion
* Class-specific Tamer Exchange Item
* Starter Digivice
* Other progression items

Some items are useful immediately, while many become more valuable once you begin running dungeons.

### Underworld Accessories

The Underworld Accessory Box contains a complete set of powerful accessories lasting **30 days**.

These accessories have fixed Digitary Power and fixed Options.

They are more than sufficient until you reach the **Radiant Holy Beast** accessory set.

> **Tip:** Don't open this box until you're ready to use it.

---

## 6. Lala's Gift Box

Lala's Gift Box contains:

* 50 Evoluters
* 10 Digimon Enhancement Buff Packages
* Novice Accessory Box
* 5 Expansion Packages
* 15 Amplification Booster +1000%

### Buffs

DMW dungeon runs commonly use:

* 8 × 1-hour buffs
* 4 × 2-hour buffs

As a general rule, save your buffs for dungeon runs.

### Novice Accessories

Novice Accessories are weaker than Underworld Accessories, but they are permanent.

You can use them during early progression.

If you previously used the **Saber-Toothed Accessories** from the tutorial, you can discard them. They cannot be upgraded and are extremely weak.

### Amplification Boosters

Amplification Booster +1000% increases EXP gained from killing enemies for 31 minutes.

They do not increase quest reward EXP.

---

## 7. Power Up Your Starter

Before continuing Elecmon's questline, go to **DATS Center**.

From Elecmon, head right and use the phone booths to teleport to DATS.

Enter the building near the back and speak with **Richard Sampson**.

Choose:

> **Go inside DATS Center**

### Clone Your Starter

Use all of the Mega Reinforced DigiClone Sets `[Savers]` on your Starter.

These Clones do not cost money.

Keep the remaining three Clones of each stat for later.

### Use Your Growth Fruit

Use the **Growth Fruit ****`[Savers]`** if you haven't already.

This increases your Starter to **125%** and allows Transcendence later.

### Level Your Starter

Use your Jumping Crystal `[Digimon]` until your Starter reaches approximately **Level 70**.

Then use the **SSS Evolution Item**.

---

## 8. Level Your Tamer

Use your Jumping Crystal `[Tamer]` to raise your Tamer to **Level 70+**.

---

## 9. Prepare Your Journey Digimon

Go to the DigiEgg Incubator and hatch your Journey DigiEgg.

Use:

* Evoluters
* Required Evolution Items

to unlock the SSS evolution you selected.

Use your remaining Jumping Crystal `[Digimon]` on the Journey Digimon to reach approximately **Level 70+**.

### LordKnightmon X

If you chose LordKnightmon X:

> **Do not use the 30-Day XAI yet.**

Save it for later.

You will eventually obtain a permanent XAI, although it is weaker.

### Clone Your Journey Digimon

Use the DigiClones from the **Journey Gift Package**.

Do not use the Clones from Lala's Gift Box.

Save the remaining Clones for HP after Transcendence.

---

## 10. Equip Your Digivice

Unlock and equip the **DMW Digivice** from your Journey Package.

It isn't fully upgraded yet.

Don't worry about Digivice enhancement for now.

---

## 11. Prepare Your Dungeon Tickets

Open the **Dungeon Ticket Box** from the tutorial.

Right-click each ticket and select **Use**.

The tickets will move into your Ticket Inventory.

The Ticket Inventory can be accessed using the **DMW** button in the bottom-right corner.

Use:

> **Claim Daily Tickets**

to claim available daily tickets without manually visiting each NPC.

---

## 12. Random BM Package

Scan the **Random BM Package** and hatch the DigiEgg you receive.

The resulting Digimon may help you complete one of your class decks.

Don't level this Digimon yet.

Your priority should remain:

1. Starter Digimon
2. Journey Digimon
3. Everything else

# 13. Attribute Advantage

DMW uses an Attribute triangle:

**Vaccine → Virus → Data → Vaccine**

* Vaccine beats Virus
* Virus beats Data
* Data beats Vaccine

There are also:

* Unknown
* No Attribute

Unknown has advantage against everything.

No Attribute is weak against everything.

### Attribute Advantage Benefits

Having Attribute Advantage provides:

* +50% damage
* 20% reduced enemy auto-attack damage
* Damage from the Basic Attribute stat on equipment

Without Attribute Advantage, your damage remains neutral and Basic Attribute damage does not apply.

Elemental Advantage provides a smaller bonus and shouldn't be prioritized yet.

---

## 14. Attribute & Element EXP

Attribute and Element EXP are shown underneath the Attribute and Element icons in the Digimon stat window.

### Attribute EXP

| Attribute | How to Level       |
| --------- | ------------------ |
| No        | Cannot be leveled  |
| Unknown   | Kill any Digimon   |
| Vaccine   | Kill Virus Digimon |
| Virus     | Kill Data Digimon  |
| Data      | Return DigiEggs    |

For Unknown, Attribute EXP is automatically 100%, but Element EXP still needs to be leveled.

For Data, the amount of Attribute/Element EXP depends on the DigiEgg being returned.

> Only Attribute or Element EXP can level at a time, even if you have enough EXP for both.

### Fast Data EXP

For quick Data EXP:

1. Visit Director Hashima in DMW Village.
2. Buy approximately 1,250 DigiEggs.
3. Return the DigiEggs.

For the other Attributes, killing Digimon around DMW Village provides a large amount of Attribute/Element EXP.

A green circle around the icon indicates that it is maxed.

Make sure both your **Starter** and **Journey Digimon** have their Attributes and Elements completed.

# 15. Begin Questing

Complete Elecmon's quest.

You'll be transported to:

**Village of the Beginning**

This begins the **File Island** questline.

Because your Tamer is now Level 70+, you should also have quests available through your Digivice.

Open the quest menu using the **Exclamation Mark** in the bottom-right corner.

Accept:

* Miki's Summons
* Commander's Urgent Summon

These lead to:

* **Glacier Area**
* **Digimon Maze / Western Area**

---

## Questing Strategy

You don't need to complete every quest.

Prioritize:

1. Main quests
2. High EXP quests
3. Sub quests when necessary
4. Daily quests when necessary

If you run out of main quests, complete sub or daily quests from the latest NPC until the next main quest becomes available.

Also check **PawnChessmon W at DATS** for Area Completion quests and rewards.

---

# 16. Seals

Start collecting Digimon Seals while questing.

Seals are cards dropped by their corresponding Digimon.

Using a **Seal Opener** unlocks the stat bonus associated with the Seal.

Press **E** to view your Seal collection.

### Early Goal

Aim for at least:

**500 seals**

for Digimon you encounter during normal questing.

Don't worry about Field Boss or Dungeon Boss Seals yet. These can be farmed more efficiently later.

### SK DPS

Prioritize:

* AT
* HP
* DS
* SK

### AA DPS

Prioritize:

* AT
* CT
* HP
* HT

Other Seal stats can be collected later.

---

# 17. Decks

Decks provide a significant amount of your overall stats and damage.

A deck requires specific Digimon to be collected.

Once you have the required Digimon, you can activate the deck.

> **Only one deck can be active at a time.**

### Minimum Recommended Deck

| Class  | Recommended Deck                 |
| ------ | -------------------------------- |
| SK DPS | Omega                            |
| AA DPS | Main Characters of the Adventure |

AA players should unlock and equip the required **Characters of the Adventure** Digimon.

### Collecting Deck Digimon

You'll encounter many DigiEggs while questing.

Some are required for your class decks.

Hatch Data can come from:

* DigiEggs marked `RANK 1/DATA`
* Direct drops from certain maps

You don't need every deck Digimon at 5/5.

Recommended:

* SSS / SSS+ Digimon → 5/5
* Other deck Digimon → 3/5 is sufficient

# 18. Recommended Quest Order

## Phase 1 — File Island

Continue the File Island questline from Elecmon.

These quests provide large amounts of EXP and should push your Starter beyond Level 100.

Don't move to Server Continent immediately.

---

## Phase 2 — Novice Challenges

Continue completing Novice Challenge quests alongside your normal progression.

By the end of File Island, you should have progressed to around:

**Koromon's Challenge - 3**

### Novice Challenge Rewards

After **Koromon's Challenge - 1**, you'll receive:

* Novice Clothes Box
* Novice Smart School Uniform
* Other rewards

The permanent **Novice Smart School Uniform** is sufficient for normal progression.

The Novice Clothes Set is also useful because you won't have a meaningful alternative clothing set yet.

---

# 19. Phase 3 — Glacier Area

Return to the Glacier Area and complete the questline started by **Miki's Summons**.

This is one of the longer questlines, but the Area Completion rewards make it worthwhile.

Continue your Novice Challenges while progressing.

### Tsunomon's Challenge - 2

This challenge requires you to visit:

**Dark Tower Wasteland**

in the Western Area.

By the end of the Glacier questline, you should have completed Tsunomon's challenges and moved onto Gabumon's.

You should also have approximately:

**15 Challenge Coins**

---

# 20. Second Starter Digimon

Return to DMW Village.

Behind the Novice Challenge NPCs, find the **Taichi NPC**.

Exchange:

**15 Challenge Coins**

for the remaining Starter Digimon for your class.

| Class  | Second Starter |
| ------ | -------------- |
| AA DPS | Lalamon        |
| SK DPS | Gaomon         |

Later, use the Growth Fruit `[Savers]` from your Area Completion rewards to bring this Starter to **125%**.

---

# 21. Phase 4 — Digimon Maze

Start the Digimon Maze questline through:

**Commander's Urgent Summon**

The Maze contains many repetitive quests, so take breaks when necessary.

If Maze becomes too tedious, you can temporarily progress into Server Continent and return later.

### Gizumon Lab

Completing the Digimon Maze questline unlocks **Gizumon Lab** at the Digimon Maze Entrance.

The dungeon provides Starter-focused rewards such as:

* `[Savers]` DigiClones
* Transcendence Chip Exchange Tickets
* Skill DigiCodes
* Other Starter progression items

After **20 successful runs**, you can exchange Sparkling Savers DigiSoul for a Starter Digimon SSS item.

### Area Completion Reward

After completing Glacier and Maze, return to **PawnChessmon W at DATS**.

Claim the Area Completion rewards.

The Growth Fruit `[Savers]` obtained here will allow your second Starter to reach 125%.

---

# 22. Phase 5 — Server Continent

Continue the File Island questline into **Server Continent**.

With a Level 100+ Digimon, this questline should be relatively easy.

By the end:

* Starter → Level 120 or close
* Journey Digimon → Level 120 or close

Continue the Novice Challenges.

You should progress from:

**Koromon's Challenge - 4**

toward:

**Greymon's Challenge - 1**

---

# 23. Transcendence

Once you've been saving your money and collecting your materials, aim for approximately:

**5T**

for your first Transcendence.

### Raremon

Return to the two Level 5 Raremon DigiEggs.

Farm an easy Devil-type Digimon and collect:

**30 Low Class Devil Data**

Use it to hatch the Raremon eggs.

### Transcend Your Starter

1. Transcend your Starter.
2. Return to Richard Sampson.
3. Clone the Starter's HP.
4. Use the remaining Starter Clones.

Once complete, you'll receive a notification confirming that your Starter is fully cloned.

Your Starter is now ready for early dungeon content.

### Journey Digimon

For your Journey Digimon, you can either:

* Buy the required Mid Class Transcendence ConvertChips from players.
* Purchase them through the Cash Shop.
* Farm the required materials.

ConvertChips can also be crafted using non-Character Bound Mid Data through Nanomon at DATS or obtained from various dungeons.

---

# 24. What to Do Next

At this point, you have two main choices:

### Option A — Continue Questing

Continue progressing through the remaining questlines.

### Option B — Begin Early Game

Start balancing quest progression with dungeon runs.

You don't need to complete every quest before beginning dungeon progression.

# 25. Optional Quest Progression

## Shinjuku

When your Digimon reaches Level 100+, you'll receive:

**"A Call"**

This sends you to Shinjuku.

Shinjuku provides large amounts of EXP and is useful for leveling your second Starter to Level 120.

After Shinjuku, you can continue into the **Digital Area**.

### Goggles

Once you reach the Digital Area, you can begin working toward **Goggles**.

Fully upgrading them costs approximately **150T**.

| Class  | Recommended Goggles |
| ------ | ------------------- |
| AA DPS | AT / AT             |
| SK DPS | SK / SK             |

---

# 26. Versandi Terminal

Richard Sampson can provide:

**"Anomalies are Detected, Investigation Required"**

This leads to **Versandi Terminal**.

### X-Program Debuff

Versandi Terminal has an X-Program debuff that:

* Deals DoT damage
* Reduces Digimon damage

The debuff can be countered by:

* Killing the Omegamon Raid in Versandi
* Using an X-Digimon
* Using an X-Protector

Killing the Omegamon Raid provides a damage buff that overrides the map-wide debuff.

### LordKnightmon X

If LordKnightmon X is your Journey Digimon, this is one situation where using your **30-Day XAI** can be worthwhile.

Later, you'll obtain access to a permanent XAI.

> You don't need to complete the Versandi questline immediately. It eventually requires **Royal Base (Normal)**, so return later when you have sufficient stats.

---

# 27. Odaiba → Spiral Mountain

You can also continue:

**Server Continent → Odaiba → Spiral Mountain**

This route continues the quest progression and Novice Challenges.

You can progress from:

**Gabumon's Challenge - 1**

toward:

**Garurumon's Challenge - 1**

### Spiral Keyring

Spiral Mountain allows you to begin collecting materials for the first level of the **Spiral Keyring**.

The Keyring becomes stronger with each upgrade.

However, later upgrades require materials from Spiral Mountain dungeons, which are endgame content.

Don't worry about those upgrades yet.

---

# 28. Early Game Preparation

Before moving into proper Early Game content, make sure you have:

## Digimon

* [ ] Starter Level 120
* [ ] Journey Digimon Level 120
* [ ] Starter Transcended
* [ ] Journey Digimon Transcended or prepared for Transcendence
* [ ] Starter fully cloned
* [ ] Journey Digimon fully cloned
* [ ] Attributes completed
* [ ] Elements completed

## Equipment

* [ ] DMW Digivice equipped
* [ ] Appropriate accessories
* [ ] Appropriate costumes

## Progression

* [ ] Novice Challenges progressing
* [ ] Seals collected
* [ ] Class deck unlocked
* [ ] Class deck active

### Required Deck

**SK DPS:** Omega

**AA DPS:** Main Characters of the Adventure

Once these requirements are met, you're ready to begin **proper Early Game progression and dungeon farming**.
