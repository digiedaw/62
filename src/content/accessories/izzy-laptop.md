## Overview

No battery life lasts forever in the Digital World, yet Izzy's laptop never dies
— much to everyone's relief. He uses it to analyze Digimon data, decode
prophecies, and poke at the fabric of the network itself.

## Notable moments

- Discovering the alphabet cipher behind the Digimon ruins
- Communicating with Gennai's holograms
- Mapping the connection between the real world and the Digital World
