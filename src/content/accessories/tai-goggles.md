## Overview

The **aviator goggles** perched on Taichi "Tai" Kamiya's head are arguably the
most recognizable accessory in the entire franchise. Practical for desert winds
and symbolic for leadership, they became the trademark of every series lead that
followed.

## Notes

- Worn on the forehead rather than the eyes — for style, apparently.
- Later protagonists (Davis, Takato, Taiki…) inherit the goggles motif.
