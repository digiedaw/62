## Ancient Gear

Ancient Gear is your main equipment during Mid Game.

Craft the following first:

- Ancient Ring Lv.10
- Ancient Necklace Lv.10
- Ancient Earring Lv.10

These accessories are required before progressing into Spiral Mountain dungeons.

---

## Ultimate Ancient Gear

Ultimate Ancient Gear is an upgraded version of Ancient Gear.

Advantages:

- Maximum stat values
- No Number Change Stones required

To upgrade, you'll need:

- Lv.10 Ancient Gear
- Matching Divine Holy Beast Gear
- 20 Fanronkou

---

## Farming Priority

Your goal should be:

1. Ancient Ring Lv.10
2. Ancient Necklace Lv.10
3. Ancient Earring Lv.10
4. Upgrade them to Ultimate Ancient Gear (Optional)

> Fanglongmon Gear is one of the largest progression milestones in DMW. Completing it unlocks most End Game content.

---

## Recommended After Completion

Once you finish your Fanglongmon Gear, you can move on to:

- Arena Hero
- Arena Extreme
- Free SSS+ Digimon progression
- Digivice upgrades
- Spiral Mountain content
