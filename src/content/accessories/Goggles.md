---# Goggles

Goggles are progression-based equipment that provide **two fixed stat bonuses** and unique stat combinations depending on their type.

Different versions of Goggles can be crafted and upgraded through **Takato (Digital Area Craft)** at **DATS Center**.

>### Getting Started
>
- Craft and upgrade Goggles through **Takato (Digital Area Craft)**.
- Most crafting materials are obtained from the **Digital Area** and related dungeons.
- Every crafting step has a **100% Success Rate**.

---

# Goggle Stats

Each pair of Goggles comes with **two fixed stat bonuses** that **cannot be changed**.

Different Goggle variants provide different stat combinations.

### Important info

- try to roll until u find double stats for 1 role, 
- Goggles stats for each role maybe can change in future since they always updating the game
- you can find best goggles stats for each role on next table
> **Attention**
- dont try to upgrade your trash combo goggles to the next level, i recomend the combo goggles at the table under for each role

### Best goggles stats

> **Skill DPS**
- Use SK/SK Goggles
> **AA DPS**
- Use AT/AT Goggles
> **Tank**
- Use DE/DE Goggles

# Crafting Goggles

All Goggles are crafted and upgraded through **Takato (Digital Area Craft)** in **DATS Center**.

>Success Rate: **100%**
