## Overview

The **Digivice** is the essential DigiDestined tool. It glows when a partner is
in danger, can force-evolve a Digimon, and doubles as a radar for other chosen
children.

## Known functions

- Triggering normal and Crest-powered evolution
- Tracking other Digivices / DigiDestined nearby
- Purifying dark gears and freeing controlled Digimon
- Storing and projecting digientry data (later models)
