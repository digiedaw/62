Digimon Arena Hero is one of the most important daily activities during Mid Game.

Besides Arena Points, Hero Mode rewards **Supreme Evolution Summon Cards**, which are required to unlock several free **SSS+ Digimon**.

---

## Why Run Hero Mode?

Running Hero Mode daily lets you:

- Earn Arena Ranking Points
- Collect Supreme Evolution Summon Cards
- Practice advanced boss mechanics
- Prepare for Arena Extreme

---

## Before You Start

Make sure you have:

- A Vaccine, Virus, and Data Digimon
- Fully cloned and leveled Digimon
- Mid Game accessories
- Basic knowledge of dungeon mechanics

> A Tank is highly recommended for the final rounds.

---

## Rewards

Rounds reward:

- Ranking Points
- Supreme Evolution Summon Card V1
- Supreme Evolution Summon Card V2

These cards are used to unlock free SSS+ Digimon such as:

- Susanoomon Shin
- Omegamon Alter-B
- Fanglongmon Shin
- Examon X

---

## Boss Mechanics

### Round 1 — Millenniummon

At **30% HP**

Drop poison circles around the edges of the arena.

Then follow this attack pattern:

- Split
- Split
- Stack
- Stack
- Stack
- Split
- Stack
- Split
- Stack
- Split

Ignoring the order will usually result in death.

---

### Rounds 2 — 4

Mechanics remain simple but bosses hit much harder.

If your HP or Defense is low, bring a Tank.

---

### Round 5 — Examon X

A Tank is required.

Field Effect:

- Constant damage over time

At roughly **55%** and **35%**

Examon X uses **Dodge This!**, a powerful party-wide attack.

At **10%**

- Slayerdramon and Breakdramon appear.
- Defeat both quickly.
- Higest damage dps get item, Use the dropped item on Examon X.

Failing this mechanic wipes the party.

---

### Round 6 — Ordinemon

A Tank is required.

Field Effect:

- Reduced Block

At **50%**

Follow this sequence:

- Stack
- Split
- Stack
- Stack
- Split
- Split

At **40%**

Most attacks target the Tank.

DPS players should stay together and focus on damage.

At **10%**

Ordinemon gains:

- Massive damage reduction
- Huge attack increase

Defeat her before your Tank dies.

---

## Tips

- Hero Mode is worth running every day.
- Learn boss mechanics instead of relying on damage.
- Don't be afraid to retry later rounds.
- Hero Mode prepares you for Arena Extreme.
