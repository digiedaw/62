Berserk Arena is one of the hardest Mid-Game challenges. You fight **two bosses at the same time** — Omegamon **Alter-S** and Omegamon **Zwart** — and they share HP percentage breakpoints. Mis-managing the meter and your team gets wiped.

---

## Recommended Stats

:::tip[Stat Requirements]

- **Tanker Digimon** — required
- **Special Chicken Combo** consumables
- **Dark Chocolate** consumables
- **10,000+ Hit Rate** for the Tanker (11,000+ to land every hit)
- **Highest Regen Level 3 / 4** to survive long fights
- **Highest Defender Level 3 / 4** to reduce incoming damage
- **Title** — *Dawn of a New Era* or *Defender of Winter 2023* to reduce damage further

:::

## Party Setup

You need **two Tanks + two damage dealers**.

| Boss you focus first | Recommended Tanker |
| --- | --- |
| Omegamon **Zwart** | **Shoutmon DX** |
| Omegamon **Alter-S** | **Icymagnamon** or **Cosmic Susanoomon** |

> Killing one boss triggers a **minion spawn** from the other. Plan the order so the surviving boss does not stack adds on your weakened team.

---

## Core Rule — Equal HP%

The two bosses share a meter. **Never let one boss drop more than ~20% below the other.**

At the following HP%, **stop hitting** until both bosses are at the same % again:

- **80%**
- **60%**
- **40%**
- **20%**

:::danger[Why this matters]
If one boss is at 79% and the other is at 81%, the lower one spawns a minion immediately. If your team keeps attacking, the HP gap widens and mechanics pile up. Pause damage, equalize, then resume.
:::

## Quick-Reference Table

| HP% | What happens | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 80 / 60 / 40 / 20 | Equalize | **Stop hitting** until both bosses match |
| 79 / 59 / 39 / 19 | Minion spawns | Kill minion first; meteors continue |
| 76 / 71 / 55 / 33 | 4x single meteor | Spread out, heal the Tank |
| 76 / 55 / 44 / 32 / 22 / 12 / 3 | **Freeze to Death!** (tank buster) | Use defensive Tamer Skill + Dark Chocolate |
| 56 / 48 / 39 / 26 / 19 / 12 / 3 | Many blue AoE circles | Move out, do not stack |

## Survival Tips

:::tip[Survival Tips]

- **Always pause damage at the equalize thresholds** (80/60/40/20). Skipping this is the #1 wipe reason.
- **Tank uses Dark Chocolate** right before each *Freeze to Death!* cast.
- **Special Chicken Combo** before the encounter for a big ATK + DEF boost.
- **Tamer Skill timing** - save your Tamer defensive skill for the tank buster windows.
- **Meteors and circles stack** in the late phases - DPS needs to be mobile.

- **Practice the meter** once your team learns the equalize rhythm, the rest is muscle memory.

:::

---

## Recommended Order

1. **First Berserk run?** Kill **Zwart** first (Shoutmon DX as Tank). Slightly easier timing.
2. **Once comfortable?** Try killing **Alter-S** first (Icymagnamon or Cosmic Susanoomon as Tank).
3. **Both strategies viable** - pick whichever your Tank is most comfortable with.
