# Fanglongmon

Fanglongmon is the main progression dungeon of the **Mid Game**.

Completing this dungeon allows you to craft **Ancient Gear**, one of the biggest upgrades before entering End Game content.

---

## Requirements

Before farming Fanglongmon, you should have:

- Radiant Holy Beast Gear (Semi-Perfect or better)
- Royal : X-Knight Bracelet
- A fully built SSS or SSS+ Digimon
- A party that understands the mechanics

---

## Dungeon Progression

You must defeat all **Four Holy Beasts** before fighting Fanglongmon.

Boss order:

1. Xuanwumon
2. Zhuqiaomon
3. Baihumon
4. Qinglongmon
5. Fanglongmon

Each Holy Beast keeps the mechanics from its Hard Mode dungeon.

---

## Holy Beast Mechanic

Each Holy Beast applies **three debuffs** that reduce your damage.

To remove them:

1. Find the three summoned subordinates.
2. Defeat all three.
3. The damage reduction is removed for the entire party.

> The debuffs will return after some time, so repeat the mechanic whenever the minions respawn.

---

## Fanglongmon Battle

After defeating Qinglongmon, you'll enter Fanglongmon's arena.

Unlike the Holy Beasts:

- No damage reduction debuffs
- Simple mechanics
- Focus on avoiding attacks and maintaining damage

The fight is much easier once everyone understands the Holy Beast mechanics.

---

## Dungeon Rewards

Fanglongmon drops materials used to craft:

- Ancient Ring
- Ancient Necklace
- Ancient Earring

It also drops:

- Fanronkou
- Divine Crystal (Rare)
