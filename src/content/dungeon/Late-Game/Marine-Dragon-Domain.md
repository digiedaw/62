Marine Dragon Domain is one of the **entry-level Late-Game dungeons** in DMW. You face a dragon boss that punishes unprepared teams with hard-hitting water mechanics.

This dungeon drops the materials used to craft the **Marine Dragon set** — a strong Water-attribute accessory set used by many AA and SK builds.

---

## Recommended Stats

### Stats for DPS

- Max HP: at least 1.2M
- ATK: 1.5M+ (or your best Damage stat)
- Crit Rate: 80%+
- Crit Damage: 220%+
- Water Resistance: 30%+ (helpful, not required)

### Stats for Tank

- Max HP: 2M+
- DEF: 1.2M+
- Block Rate: 60%+
- Block Damage Reduction: 70%+
- Recovery / Regen: highest available

---

## What You Need

- **4 DPS Digimon** (any attribute works, but **Water resist** recommended)
- **1 Tank Digimon** with high HP + Block
- Tamer with **defensive cooldown** for the Tank buster (e.g. Henry, Joe, Taichi)
- **Dark Chocolate** consumables for the Tank
- **Special Chicken Combo** consumables for DPS

## Boss Mechanics

Marine Dragon uses a **cycle of three attacks** that escalate in damage. The Tank should hold aggro and use defensive cooldowns on the marked cast.

### Phase 1 — Tidal Slam

- **Single-target hit** on the Tank.
- Light AoE splash on the Tank's position.
- Easily blocked.

### Phase 2 — Whirlpool (cast bar appears)

- Massive **circular AoE** centered on the boss.
- **All players** must move out to the edge.
- Stand on the rim — the safe zone is the outer ring.
- The Tank can re-engage after the cast finishes.

### Phase 3 — Water Beam (tank buster)

- Long cast bar, the boss locks on the Tank.
- **Tank buster** — deals massive single-target damage.
- :::tip[Counter]
-  Tank uses **Dark Chocolate** before the cast finishes.
-  Tamer activates a **defensive cooldown** (Henry Guard, Joe Impregnableness, Taichi Vigor).
-  Party members **do not** stand in front of the boss during this cast — cleave damage kills DPS.
-  :::

### Phase 4 — Tidal Slam (repeat)

- The cycle restarts. The Tank re-establishes aggro.
- DPS resumes once the Tank has the boss facing away.

---

## Fight Phases by HP%

| HP% | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 100% - 75% | Phase 1 + 2 (Tidal Slam + Whirlpool) | Tank builds aggro. DPS focuses boss. Avoid Whirlpool AoE. |
| 75% - 50% | Phase 3 (Water Beam) added to cycle | Save Tamer cooldown for the **first** Water Beam. Pop Dark Chocolate. |
| 50% - 25% | Cycle speeds up (~15% faster) | Tank uses cooldowns **every other** Water Beam. DPS stays mobile. |
| 25% - 0% | Whirlpool becomes larger, AoE adds stacks | Keep moving. Don't stack. Burn the boss. |

---

:::tip[Survival Tips]
- **Always face the boss away from the party** after re-engaging — Water Beam has a frontal cone.
- **Do not stand in the Whirlpool** — even at the edge, the inner part does heavy damage.
- **Tank cooldowns are precious** — don't waste them on Tidal Slam; save for Water Beam.
- **DPS stays behind the boss** to avoid cleave and to be ready to dodge Whirlpool.
- **First Water Beam is the check** — if your Tank survives it, you clear the dungeon.
:::

## Loot & Rewards

Each clear drops:

- **Marine Dragon Scale** x3-5 (used to craft the Marine Dragon accessory set)
- **Water-attribute Digivolution materials**
- **Gold** (based on clear time)
- **EXP Boost Tokens** (small chance)

The **Marine Dragon set** is a solid mid-late-game accessory set for Water and Neutral DPS builds.

---

## Tips for Faster Clears

- **Bring Fire or Light Digimon** for the elemental advantage — clears are 20-30% faster.
- **Use a Tank with high Block Rate** — the Water Beam is fully blockable.
- **Save your Tamer's defensive skill** for the first Water Beam at 75% HP.
- **Speed-run setups** can skip the Whirlpool with high mobility skills (e.g. JetMervamon).
