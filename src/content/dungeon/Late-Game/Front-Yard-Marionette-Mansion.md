The Front Yard Marionette Mansion is a **Late-Game dungeon** focused on the puppet-themed Marionette boss. The dungeon rewards materials used to craft the **Marionette accessory set** — a strong Dark-attribute set for AA and SK builds.

The encounter is built around **puppet mechanics** that confuse positioning, so the team must coordinate carefully.

---

## Recommended Stats

### Stats for DPS

- Max HP: 1.3M+
- ATK: 1.5M+ (or your best Damage stat)
- Crit Rate: 80%+
- Crit Damage: 230%+
- Dark Resistance: 30%+ (recommended)

### Stats for Tank

- Max HP: 2M+
- DEF: 1.2M+
- Block Rate: 70%+
- Block Damage Reduction: 70%+
- Recovery / Regen: highest available

---

## What You Need

- **3 Vaccine DPS Digimon** (recommended)
- **1 Vaccine Tank Digimon**
- **Yoshino Tamer** for DPS (uses Protection Tamer Skill to survive AoE)
- **Kyoshiro Tamer** for Tank (Overflow Tamer Skill + high Regen)
- **Dark Chocolate** consumables for the Tank
- **Special Chicken Combo** consumables for DPS

## Boss Mechanics

Marionette uses a **confusion-based rotation** that mixes AoE, single-target, and clone mechanics. The Tank holds aggro while the team dodges AoE and avoids the clones.

### Phase 1 — String Snap
- **Single-target hit** on the Tank.
- Light AoE splash — easy to ignore.

### Phase 2 — Marionette Strings (cast bar appears)
- **Puppet strings** attach to **3 random party members**.
- :::tip[How to handle]
-  Affected players are **slowed** and **cannot use movement skills** for 8 seconds.
-  Tank should NOT be one of the affected players — keep aggro clean.
-  Wait for the debuff to expire naturally; do not waste cooldowns on it.
-  :::

### Phase 3 — Dark Burst (tank buster)
- Long cast bar; the boss locks onto the Tank.
- **Massive single-target damage** that ignores Block partially.
- :::tip[Counter]
-  Tank uses **Dark Chocolate** *and* **Special Chicken Combo** before the cast finishes.
-  Kyoshiro Tamer pops **Overflow** Tamer Skill (massive DEF + regen for 10s).
-  DPS should not be in front of the boss — cleave damage kills.
-  :::

### Phase 4 — Mirror Image
- Boss spawns **2 mirror clones** at 70% / 50% / 30% HP.
- Clones share HP, deal moderate damage, and **disappear after 30 seconds**.
- :::tip[How to handle]
-  **Focus fire the REAL boss**, not the clones. Clones despawn.
-  Do not waste your burst on clones — they're a distraction.
-  Tank holds aggro on the real boss; clones have their own aggro.
-  :::

## Fight Phases by HP%

| HP% | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 100% - 75% | Phase 1 + 2 (String Snap + Marionette Strings) | Tank builds aggro. DPS focuses boss. Avoid clumping so 3 different players get strings. |
| 75% - 50% | Phase 3 (Dark Burst) added | Save Tamer cooldown for the **first** Dark Burst. Pop consumables. |
| 70% / 50% / 30% | Mirror Image spawns (2 clones) | **Ignore the clones**. Continue DPS on the real boss. |
| 50% - 25% | Cycle speeds up (~15% faster) | Tank uses cooldowns **every other** Dark Burst. |
| 25% - 0% | Marionette Strings hits 4 players at once | Burn the boss. Don't panic if everyone is slowed. |

---


:::tip[Survival Tips]
- **Bring Vaccine** — Dark-attribute damage from the boss is reduced by ~30% on Vaccine Digimon.
- **Do NOT attack the mirror clones** — they despawn after 30 seconds; burst the real boss.
- **Spread out at ~75% HP** — Marionette Strings will hit 3 random players, and you don't want 2 strings on the Tank.
- **Tank uses both consumables** before every Dark Burst (chocolate + chicken combo).
- **Kyoshiro's Overflow** is the strongest defensive Tamer Skill for the Tank — use it on the first Dark Burst.
- **DPS stays behind the boss** to avoid cleave from Dark Burst and the AoE splash.
- **If you wipe at 30%** — it's almost always because the team is chasing clones. Stay on the real boss.
:::

## Loot & Rewards

Each clear drops:

- **Marionette String** x3-5 (used to craft the Marionette accessory set)
- **Dark-attribute Digivolution materials**
- **Gold** (based on clear time)
- **EXP Boost Tokens** (small chance)

The **Marionette set** is a strong Dark-resist set used by many Dark-attribute and Tank builds.

---

## Tips for Faster Clears

- **Bring 4 Vaccine DPS** for the elemental resist — clears are 20-30% faster.
- **Use a Tank with Block Damage Reduction 70%+** — Dark Burst is partially blockable.
- **Save Kyoshiro's Overflow** for the first Dark Burst at 75% HP.
- **Ignore the clones completely** — most wipes come from chasing them.
- **Yoshino on DPS** is highly recommended — her Protection Tamer Skill saves lives during AoE phases.
